**Session Goal:**  
The session aimed to explore the technical substrates in data analysis, focusing on dimensionality reduction and its applications in bridging structured and textual data. Additionally, it examined the contributions of María Vanina Martínez in logic and NLP, and Santiago Laplagne in computational mathematics.  

**Key Activities:**  
- Discussed the technical aspects of dimensionality reduction on structured data and its cross-disciplinary applications with NLP.  
- Analyzed María Vanina Martínez's work on logic, inconsistency handling, and probabilistic reasoning, highlighting its relevance to NLP and dimensionality reduction.  
- Decomposed Martínez's methods for handling inconsistent tabular data and their application to socioeconomic data problems.  
- Reflected on Santiago Laplagne's contributions to computational mathematics, focusing on algebraic geometry and its applications in biology and optimization.  
- Identified potential collaborative groups for research on poverty measurement, data analysis, and NLP.  

**Achievements:**
