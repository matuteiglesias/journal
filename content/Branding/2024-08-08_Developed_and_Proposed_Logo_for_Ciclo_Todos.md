## 📅 2024-08-08 — Session: Developed and Proposed Logo for Ciclo Todos

**🕒 16:30–16:55**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Logo Design, Branding, Diversity, Inclusion, Graphic Design  
**📂 Project**: Branding  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


### Session Goal
The session aimed to develop and propose a logo design for the community group 'Ciclo Todos', focusing on diversity, inclusion, and unity.

### Key Activities
- Explored various design concepts for the logo, incorporating elements of diversity and unity.
- Discussed the inclusion of Brazilian cultural elements and vibrant color palettes.
- Proposed multiple design ideas featuring stylized bicycles with wheels representing diversity through different skin tones.
- Considered the need for adaptable designs suitable for various formats, including color and black-and-white versions.

### Achievements
- Developed a comprehensive creative brief for the logo design.
- Proposed a logo concept that symbolizes diversity and unity, incorporating Brazilian cultural influences.
- Generated feedback on the proposed designs to refine and finalize the logo.

### Pending Tasks
- Gather and analyze feedback on the proposed logo designs.
- Refine the logo design based on feedback and finalize the design for implementation.
