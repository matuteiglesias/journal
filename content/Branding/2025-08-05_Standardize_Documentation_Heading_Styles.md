## 📅 2025-08-05 — Session: Standardize Documentation Heading Styles

**🕒 11:10–11:20**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Formatting, Style Guide, Documentation, Text Formatting, Capitalization  
**📂 Project**: Branding  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


### Session Goal
The session aimed to address inconsistencies in heading styles within documentation, specifically focusing on the transition from sentence-case to title-case headings.

### Key Activities
- Reflected on the current use of sentence-case headings and their impact on text formatting, noting how it affects capitalization after periods.
- Discussed the benefits of title-case headings for consistency with previous documentation standards.
- Offered examples of how headings could be reformatted from sentence-case to title-case to align with established style guides.

### Achievements
- Clarified the impact of sentence-case headings on overall text appearance and consistency.
- Established a clear plan to reformat headings to title-case for improved consistency and adherence to style guides.

### Pending Tasks
- Implement the reformatting of existing documentation headings from sentence-case to title-case to ensure consistency across all documents.
