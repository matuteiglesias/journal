## 📅 2023-12-12 — Session: Developed IT Security and Administrative Protocols

**🕒 03:00–05:30**  
**🏷️ Labels**: It Security, Administrative Procedures, Vpn Access, Data Integration, Cdo Role  
**📂 Project**: Business  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


### Session Goal
The session aimed to develop comprehensive IT security protocols and administrative procedures for accessing sensitive data and systems.

### Key Activities
- **IT Team Structure**: Proposed a 20-person IT team structure for a state office in Argentina, focusing on technologies like .NET and PHP.
- **Data [[Integration]] Skills**: Reflected on essential skills for Data [[Integration]] Specialists, emphasizing ETL, middleware, and [[API]] security.
- **CDO Role and Structure**: Planned the role of Chief Data Officer (CDO) in the Ministry of Security, focusing on data analysis and resource optimization. Also, expanded the CDO team structure with a focus on data visualization and communication.
- **VPN Access and Security**: Developed a guide for legal and technical steps to obtain VPN access for intelligence advisory roles, including security policies.
- **Data Security Practices**: Executed instructions for securely working with ministry datasets, ensuring compliance with data norms.
- **Administrative Procedures**: Planned and executed formal procedures for requesting VPN and dataset access, adhering to Decreto-ley 7647/1970.

### Achievements
- Defined a structured IT team model for state offices.
- Clarified the skills needed for data integration roles.
- Established a framework for the CDO's role and team structure.
- Produced comprehensive guidelines for VPN access and data security.
- Developed formal procedures for administrative compliance in data access.

### Pending Tasks
- Further refine the IT team structure proposal based on feedback.
- Implement the proposed data security practices in a real-world scenario.
