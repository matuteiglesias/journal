## 📅 2023-12-15 — Session: Developed collaborative agendas for data-driven public safety

**🕒 15:45–17:15**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Collaboration, Data Analysis, Public Safety, Strategic Planning, Training  
**📂 Project**: Business  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


**Session Goal:**
The session aimed to develop collaborative agendas between the Data Subsecretariat and various provincial departments to enhance public safety and operational efficiency through data-driven strategies.

**Key Activities:**
- Outlined collaboration objectives and mechanisms between the Data Subsecretariat and the Provincial Police, focusing on community safety and judicial coordination.
- Developed agendas for collaboration with various subsecretariats and directorates, including Criminal Investigations, Training, Emergencies, Logistics, and Digital [[Integration]].
- Emphasized the integration of data analysis into police operations, training programs, and emergency management.
- Proposed strategies for optimizing technological integration, improving biometric identification, and ensuring data quality.

**Achievements:**
- Established comprehensive agendas detailing collaboration goals and mechanisms for various public safety initiatives.
- Identified key areas for data analysis integration to improve operational efficiency and strategic planning.

**Pending Tasks:**
- Finalize the implementation plans for each collaborative agenda, ensuring alignment with strategic goals.
- Develop detailed action plans for training and capacity building in data analysis and technology integration.
