## 📅 2023-12-15 — Session: Developed organizational and collaboration frameworks for IT

**🕒 14:20–14:50**  
**🏷️ Labels**: It Management, Data Integration, Organizational Structure, Collaboration, Strategic Planning  
**📂 Project**: Business  



### Session Goal
The session aimed to explore and develop frameworks for organizational and collaboration structures within the Ministry of Security, focusing on IT management and data [[integration]].

### Key Activities
- Reviewed the roles and responsibilities of the General Directorate of Informatics, focusing on IT resource management, software development, and data security.
- Analyzed key functions of the IT Directorate, including resource supervision, policy enforcement, and technical support provision.
- Created a Graphviz diagram to illustrate the organizational structure of the Data Subsecretariat, highlighting the Chief Data Officer's role.
- Developed a collaborative [[integration]] plan between the Technology and Data Subsecretaries, detailing teamwork mechanisms, decision-making processes, and risk management.
- Outlined an implementation plan for a new organizational structure, including candidate selection, induction programs, and strategies for smooth transitions.
- Compiled support documents for the Data Subsecretariat, covering policies, procedures, training, and development materials.
- Planned a bilateral agenda between the Data Subsecretariat and the Provincial Directorate of [[Strategic Planning]], focusing on [[data management]] and strategic security planning.

### Achievements
- Established a comprehensive understanding of IT management roles and responsibilities.
- Developed a strategic framework for collaboration and [[integration]] between key departments.
- Created actionable plans for organizational structure implementation and [[strategic planning]].

### Pending Tasks
- Finalize and approve the collaborative [[integration]] plan and implementation [[strategy]].
- Conduct training sessions based on the compiled support documents.
