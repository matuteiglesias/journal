**🏷️ Labels**: Contabilidad, Finanzas, Excel, Gastos, Deudas

**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


### Session Goal
The session aimed to develop a comprehensive system for organizing and tracking family and personal financial transactions using Excel.

### Key Activities
- Proposed a system to classify financial transactions among family members, including categorization of expenses, income, and debt distribution.
- Provided a detailed summary of personal debts and expenses, including taxes and payments made by family members.
- Designed a detailed Excel structure for organizing financial information, with sheets for overall summaries and specific expense details.
- Offered instructions for structuring a spreadsheet to monitor monthly income, expenses, and debts, particularly useful in variable cash flow contexts like rent.
- Described recommended columns for a financial tracking spreadsheet in Excel.
- Provided a formula for calculating personal accumulated balance, considering total income, personal expenses, and family debts.

### Achievements
- Successfully outlined a comprehensive Excel-based system for tracking and managing family and personal finances.
