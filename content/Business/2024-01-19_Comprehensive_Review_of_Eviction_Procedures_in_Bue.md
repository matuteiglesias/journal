### Session Goal
The session aimed to explore and understand the eviction procedures in Buenos Aires, focusing on the legal framework and strategic considerations.

### Key Activities
- Detailed review of the Civil and Commercial Procedural Code of Buenos Aires regarding eviction procedures, including relevant articles and the importance of legal consultation.
- Summarized key articles regulating eviction processes, such as types of trials, property handover, and conditions for eviction due to non-payment.
- Discussed essential questions for eviction strategy, covering property details, occupant relations, prior actions, documentation, special circumstances, client objectives, legal background, and negotiation readiness.
- Described the summary trial procedure in Buenos Aires, including demand presentation, defendant notification, response, evidence stage, hearing, and sentencing, with appeal possibilities.
- Explored strategies for managing notification evasion by occupants and legal recommendations for proceeding.
- Provided insights into the potential duration of eviction trials and strategies for efficient processing, including demand preparation, deadline compliance, and lawyer communication.
- Explained the execution of judgments and appeal procedures in eviction trials.
- Highlighted the importance of ethics in judicial processes and legitimate strategies for legal case management.
- Described the critical stages of eviction processes and the roles of judges and prosecutors.
- Offered guidelines for interacting with judicial officers and judges, emphasizing ethics and respect.
- Provided a detailed summary of the eviction process for family properties, including mediation closure and next legal steps.

### Achievements
- Comprehensive understanding of the eviction procedures in Buenos Aires.
- Clarified the roles of various legal actors and stages in the eviction process.
