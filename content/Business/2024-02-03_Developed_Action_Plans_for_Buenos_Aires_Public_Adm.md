## 📅 2024-02-03 — Session: Developed Action Plans for Buenos Aires Public Administration

**🕒 23:05–00:00**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Public Administration, Cybersecurity, Interoperability, SINDMA, Buenos Aires  
**📂 Project**: Business  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


### Session Goal
The session aimed to develop comprehensive action plans and procedural guidelines to enhance the efficiency and security of public administration systems in Buenos Aires.

### Key Activities
- **Normative Framework Analysis**: Reviewed the Decreto 875/16 to establish a normative framework for information systems management in the provincial administration.
- **Cybersecurity Collaboration**: Planned cooperation with CSIRT-PBA to enhance cybersecurity management.
- **Electronic [[Integration]] Actions**: Outlined steps for aligning with provincial regulations on electronic document processing.
- **SINDMA Implementation Plan**: Structured actions for implementing the Malvinas Argentinas Information System.
- **Electronic File Procedures**: Detailed procedures for using electronic files in public administration.
- **Service and Maintenance Procedures**: Described procedures for service payments and maintenance.
- **Data Infrastructure [[Strategy]]**: Proposed strategic actions for geospatial data infrastructure collaboration.
- **Interoperability Proposals**: Developed proposals for improving interoperability in the Ministry of Security.

### Achievements
- Established a detailed framework for various administrative processes and collaborations, focusing on improving efficiency, security, and interoperability.

### Pending Tasks
- Further development and execution of detailed action plans for each outlined proposal, especially regarding cybersecurity measures and interoperability standards.
