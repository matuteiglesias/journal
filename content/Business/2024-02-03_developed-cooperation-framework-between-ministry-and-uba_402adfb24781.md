## Description

### Session Goal
The session aimed to develop a structured framework for cooperation between the Ministry of Security of Buenos Aires and the University of Buenos Aires (UBA), focusing on technology development, training, and interoperability.

### Key Activities
- Outlined steps for establishing a cooperation agreement between the Ministry and UBA, emphasizing technology and training.
- Estimated costs for a training scheme, including classroom expenses, travel, bonuses, and payments to student instructors, totaling USD 20,100.
- Proposed strategies for interoperability in the General Directorate of Informatics to enhance system efficiency and security.
- Discussed the implications of Law 12475 on administrative transparency and interoperability.
- Detailed the implementation of compliance tools in Google Cloud Platform (GCP) to meet Law 12475 requirements.
- Explored strategies for digital transformation and process management in public administration, including the impact of the GDEBA system.

### Achievements
- Developed a comprehensive framework for cooperation agreements, addressing both educational and technological aspects.
- Established a financial outline for training initiatives.
- Identified key strategies for improving interoperability and compliance with legal standards.

### Pending Tasks
- Finalize the cooperation agreement with specific timelines and responsibilities.
- Implement the proposed interoperability strategies and compliance tools in GCP.
- Monitor the impact of the GDEBA system on administrative processes and adjust plans accordingly.
