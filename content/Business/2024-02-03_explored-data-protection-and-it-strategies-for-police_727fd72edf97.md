## Description

### Session Goal
The session focused on exploring data protection practices and IT strategies for police forces in Argentina, with an emphasis on compliance with legal frameworks and technological advancements.

### Key Activities
- Reviewed key data protection practices for provincial police in Argentina, emphasizing compliance with Ley 25.326.
- Discussed the implementation of 'sede electrónica' in police operations to enhance [[data management]] and citizen participation.
- Reflected on global technological implementations in law enforcement, highlighting systems like bike registration and online crime reporting.
- Outlined cybersecurity strategies for provincial police IT departments to prevent incidents and ensure data security.
- Analyzed the impact of Ley 14214 on [[data management]] and IT responsibilities in Buenos Aires police departments.
- Addressed document digitization and management according to relevant security and accessibility norms.
