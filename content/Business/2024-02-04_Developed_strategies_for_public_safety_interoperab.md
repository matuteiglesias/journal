## 📅 2024-02-04 — Session: Developed strategies for public safety interoperability

**🕒 00:00–03:00**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Interoperability, Public Safety, Technology Integration, Collaboration, Digital Transformation  
**📂 Project**: Business  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


### Session Goal:
The session aimed to explore and develop strategies for enhancing public safety through improved interoperability between governmental and private entities.

### Key Activities:
- Discussed potential collaborations between the Ministry of Security's IT Directorate and various public and private entities to enhance interoperability and public safety.
- Proposed integration of CCTV feeds with police forces, considering technological solutions, legal requirements, and privacy concerns.
- Reflected on U.S. initiatives allowing security forces access to private security camera feeds, highlighting technologies, legal considerations, and practical implementation methods.
- Reviewed Project Green Light in Detroit as a case study for public-private-community partnerships in enhancing neighborhood safety.
- Listed key documents and IT systems crucial for police operations, emphasizing their role in information management and emergency response.
- Proposed digital training initiatives for security personnel in Buenos Aires, including e-learning platforms and mobile apps.
- Detailed actions for implementing and managing the Single Administration System (SUAD), covering system integration, information security, and user management.
- Reflected on strategies to strengthen police security in Buenos Aires through unified databases and advanced information systems.
- Described the multidisciplinary team needed for implementing mobile app functionalities for sworn declarations.
- Proposed a digital approach for fulfilling sworn declarations by police personnel, emphasizing collaboration and secure mobile application development.
- Suggested updates to 2007 regulations to incorporate digital tools and information security practices, focusing on data privacy and interoperability.

### Achievements:
- Developed a comprehensive understanding of the current landscape of public safety interoperability.
- Identified key areas for technological integration and collaboration between public and private sectors.
- Formulated actionable proposals and frameworks for enhancing public safety through technology.

### Pending Tasks:
- Further exploration of legal frameworks and privacy implications for integrating private security feeds with public safety forces.
- Development of detailed implementation plans for proposed digital training and mobile application initiatives.
