## 📅 2024-02-08 — Session: Conducted SWOT and ISO 9001 planning for Police IT

**🕒 19:30–20:10**  
**🏷️ Labels**: SWOT, Quality Management, ISO 9001, Strategic Planning, Police It  
**📂 Project**: Business  



### Session Goal:
The session aimed to conduct a SWOT analysis and plan for the implementation of a Quality Management System (QMS) aligned with ISO 9001:2015 standards for the Dirección General de Informática within a police force.

### Key Activities:
- Conducted a SWOT analysis to identify strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats related to the IT department and its [[strategic planning]] for a Quality Management System.
- Defined the scope of the QMS, including organizational context, services, stakeholder expectations, and compliance with ISO 9001:2015.
- Outlined a strategic plan for launching the QMS, detailing phases of planning, implementation, and continuous improvement.
- Developed a phased [[strategy]] for achieving ISO 9001 compliance within two years, focusing on foundation building, process [[optimization]], and sustainability.

### Achievements:
- Completed a comprehensive SWOT analysis for the IT department.
- Established a clear scope and strategic plan for the QMS implementation.
- Developed a phased approach for ISO 9001 compliance.

### Pending Tasks:
- Further refinement of the strategic plan to ensure alignment with organizational goals and stakeholder expectations.
- Implementation of the outlined phases and continuous monitoring for improvements.
