## 📅 2024-02-15 — Session: Explored Agile Project Management Techniques

**🕒 18:15–18:45**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Agile, Project Management, Estimation Techniques, Collaboration, Story Points  
**📂 Project**: Business  



### Session Goal
The session aimed to explore various aspects of Agile [[project management]], focusing on improving project planning and estimation techniques.

### Key Activities
- **Reflection on Traditional Project Planning:** Analyzed common pitfalls in traditional project planning and emphasized a customer-centric approach.
- **Agile Methodologies Exploration:** Discussed the effectiveness and flexibility of Agile methodologies compared to traditional methods, highlighting key principles, roles, and planning strategies.
- **Agile Estimation Techniques:** Examined how Agile teams separate size and duration estimates using story points and team velocity, and introduced the concept of 'ideal days' for measuring task size and duration.
- **Collaborative Estimation Techniques:** Reviewed various estimation techniques in Agile projects, such as Planning Poker, and stressed the importance of collaboration in achieving accurate estimates.
- **Comparison of Estimation Methods:** Compared the benefits of story points and ideal days, recommending a gradual adoption of story points for teams struggling with abstract estimations.

### Achievements
- Gained insights into the limitations of traditional project planning and the advantages of Agile methodologies.
- Clarified the use of story points and ideal days in Agile estimation, enhancing understanding of their application in project planning.

### Pending Tasks
- Further exploration of how to integrate Agile estimation techniques into existing [[project management]] practices.
- Develop strategies for teams to transition smoothly to Agile estimation methods, particularly in environments resistant to change.
