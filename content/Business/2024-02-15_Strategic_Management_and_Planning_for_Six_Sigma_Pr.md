### Session Goal
The session aimed to explore strategic management and planning techniques for Six Sigma projects, focusing on enhancing project success through effective resource allocation, stakeholder analysis, and cross-functional collaboration.

### Key Activities
- **Strategic Management of Six Sigma Projects:** Discussed the importance of customer competitiveness, stakeholder impact, and effective resource allocation.
- **[[Project Management]] Strategies:** Explored single-tasking, critical chain project management, and management support to overcome roadblocks.
- **Cross-Functional Collaboration:** Highlighted communication, project tracking, and team recognition within hierarchical organizations.
- **Synchronization Resources:** Introduced the concept of synchronization resources for improved coordination in project planning.
- **Benchmarking in 14-Week Plans:** Detailed a strategic approach for implementing benchmarking principles, including planning, analysis, and action phases.
- **KPIs for IT Management:** Proposed KPIs for evaluating operational performance and strategic impact in IT management.

### Achievements
- Clarified strategic approaches for managing and planning Six Sigma projects.
- Developed a framework for integrating synchronization resources and benchmarking into project plans.
- Proposed KPIs for IT management to enhance performance evaluation.
