### Session Goal
The session focused on managing [[documentation]] and project planning within IT systems, emphasizing the use of electronic document management platforms like GEDO and GDEBA.

### Key Activities
- Detailed the structured process for finalizing and implementing IT documents, including review, approval, digital signing, archiving, and [[communication]].
- Planned document management processes for new policy implementations, covering training material generation, effective distribution, and post-implementation monitoring.
- Developed a [[project management]] plan using the Expediente Electrónico (EE) on GDEBA, integrating it into project phases for enhanced efficiency and transparency.
- Explored advanced functionalities in GDEBA for managing electronic files, including task supervision and handling confidential content.
- Outlined roles and responsibilities in critical projects within the Ministry of Security, focusing on [[strategic planning]] and resource allocation.

### Achievements
- Established a comprehensive framework for document and [[project management]] in IT, integrating electronic systems for improved [[workflow]] and security.
