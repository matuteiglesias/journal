## 📅 2024-02-22 — Session: Developed Legal Strategies for Property Recovery

**🕒 20:00–21:05**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Legal, Property Recovery, Eviction, Usucapion, Judicial Process  
**📂 Project**: Business  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


### Session Goal:
The session aimed to explore and develop comprehensive legal strategies for property recovery, focusing on eviction, usucapion, and preventive embargo within Buenos Aires' legal framework.

### Key Activities:
- **Legal Insight Compilation**: Reviewed legal aspects of eviction due to non-payment, inheritance management, and restitution actions.
- **Norms Analysis**: Analyzed legal norms related to eviction, usucapion, and preventive embargo, identifying relevant articles and application conditions.
- **[[Strategy]] Formulation**: Outlined key legal questions to formulate an effective property recovery strategy, including necessary documentation and tenant communication.
- **Judicial Process Guidance**: Provided guidance on judicial processes, detailing responsibilities of plaintiffs and lawyers, and the application of specific legislation such as Article 330.
- **Demand Structuring**: Offered templates for legal demands, emphasizing the importance of detailing facts, applicable rights, and specific petitions.

### Achievements:
- Compiled a comprehensive guide on legal strategies for property recovery.
- Clarified the legal framework and necessary steps for eviction and property recovery processes.

### Pending Tasks:
- Further customization of legal demand templates with specific case data.
- Exploration of additional legal articles that may impact the strategy.
