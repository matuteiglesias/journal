## Description

### Session Goal
The session aimed to explore and develop strategies for resolving conflicts and fostering inclusive leadership within groups.

### Key Activities
- **Conflict Resolution and Leadership**: Discussed strategies for recognizing group dynamics, encouraging reflection, and offering support to facilitate conflict resolution and strengthen commitment and collaboration.
- **Political Analysis**: Analyzed the neglect of Kirchnerist and Peronist values such as inclusion, unity, and respect for diversity in current conflicts, and discussed their application in group dynamics.
- **Group Dynamics**: Highlighted key ideas and phrases from Peronism and Kirchnerism to promote group cohesion and effectiveness, focusing on loyalty, social justice, and participation.
- **Political Sovereignty**: Explored the principle of political sovereignty in groups, emphasizing inclusion, independence, and justice in decision-making.
- **Communication Coaching**: Provided techniques for effective voice management and body posture to enhance communication and inclusivity.

### Achievements
- Developed a comprehensive set of strategies for inclusive leadership and conflict resolution.
- Integrated political principles into group dynamics to enhance cohesion and effectiveness.
- Enhanced communication skills through coaching techniques.
