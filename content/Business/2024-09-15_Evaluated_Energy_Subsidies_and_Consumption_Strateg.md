## 📅 2024-09-15 — Session: Evaluated Energy Subsidies and Consumption Strategies

**🕒 04:50–06:50**

**📂 Project**: Business  



### Session Goal
The primary goal of this session was to evaluate energy subsidies related to electricity tariffs and analyze energy consumption patterns to provide actionable recommendations for reducing energy usage.

### Key Activities
- **Subsidy Evaluation:** Reviewed guidelines to determine eligibility for electricity tariff subsidies based on household income, property ownership, and vehicle possession.
- **Energy Consumption Analysis:** Analyzed user energy consumption and confirmed the application of state subsidies on utility bills. Suggested energy-saving actions.
- **Recommendations for Electric Heaters:** Provided practical advice on reducing high energy consumption from electric heaters, including [[optimization]] tips, thermal insulation, alternative heating methods, and programmable thermostats.
- **Appliance Energy Consumption:** Listed high-energy-consuming appliances and calculated the cost of a 60W bulb.

### Achievements
- Clarified the criteria for electricity tariff subsidy eligibility.
- Developed a set of recommendations to optimize energy usage in households.

### Pending Tasks
- Further exploration of alternative energy-efficient appliances and lighting solutions.
- Detailed comparison of LED bulb models for warm white light to finalize recommendations.
