- **Debt Tracking Table Structure**: Proposed a table format to organize information on locations, taxes, and outstanding balances.
- **Debt Management Analysis**: Provided insights into debt accumulation, rent collection, and payment strategies.
- **Exploration of Financial Accountability**: Emphasized the importance of understanding financial management principles beyond tool reliance.
- **Financial Principles Reflection**: Outlined principles of cash flow management, debt management, budgeting, and investment strategies.
- **Financial Planning Framework**: Detailed a six-month plan focusing on debt reduction, savings, and cash flow management.
