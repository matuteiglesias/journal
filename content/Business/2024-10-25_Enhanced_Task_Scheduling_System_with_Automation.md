### Session Goal
The session aimed to refine and enhance the task scheduling system by incorporating advanced features such as 'Estimated Duration' and 'Recurrence', along with a prioritization structure for different times of the day.

- **Refinement of Task Scheduling System**: Planned enhancements to include columns for 'Estimated Duration' and 'Recurrence'.

- **Task Classification Examples**: Provided examples for categorizing tasks into types like Urgent, Important, Routine, etc.

### Achievements

- Developed a clear structure for task categorization and prioritization.
