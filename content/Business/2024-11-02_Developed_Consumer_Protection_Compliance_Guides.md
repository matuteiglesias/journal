## 📅 2024-11-02 — Session: Developed Consumer Protection Compliance Guides

**🕒 14:50–15:05**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Consumer Protection, Legal Compliance, Documentation, Transaction Receipts, Mercado Pago

### Session Goal:
The session aimed to develop comprehensive guides and templates for ensuring compliance with consumer protection laws, particularly focusing on transaction [[documentation]] and legal recourse.

### Key Activities:
1. **Exploration of Consumer Protection Legislation:**
   - Reviewed Article 4 of the Consumer Defense Law, emphasizing the obligation of providers to offer clear and detailed information to consumers.
2. **Instruction on Obtaining Transaction Receipts:**
   - Provided a detailed guide on how to obtain transaction receipts from COELSA and Mercado Pago, including necessary steps and legal considerations.
3. **[[Documentation]] for AGIP Compliance:**
   - Created instructions to align [[documentation]] for AGIP with information from Mercado Pago and COELSA, including steps for obtaining receipts and drafting explanatory notes.
4. **Template for Filing Complaints:**
   - Developed a template for filing complaints against Mercado Pago under the Consumer Defense Law, detailing legal steps and arguments.
5. **Analysis of Transaction Receipt Issues:**
   - Analyzed issues related to missing transaction receipts and discussed applicable legislation and response strategies.

### Achievements:
- Developed comprehensive guides and templates for consumer protection compliance.
- Clarified legal obligations and procedures for obtaining transaction receipts.
