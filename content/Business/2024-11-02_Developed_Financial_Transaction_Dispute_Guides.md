**🕒 16:20–16:35**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Financial Disputes, Credit Card, Mercado Pago, Refunds, Legal Claims

### Session Goal
The session aimed to develop comprehensive guides for handling disputes related to financial transactions, specifically focusing on cancellations and refunds involving credit cards and Mercado Pago.

### Key Activities
- Created a detailed guide for canceling payments made with credit cards and requesting refunds, including steps to follow with the issuing bank, Mastercard, and additional claim options.
- Developed a guide for filing refund claims with banks and Mastercard due to insufficient [[documentation]] from Mercado Pago, outlining steps for situation review, bank contact, and escalation to Mastercard or consumer protection.
- Provided a detailed procedure for canceling transactions via Mercado Pago and credit cards, specifying necessary steps and [[documentation]].
- Detailed options for managing claims related to payments via Mercado Pago, including requesting complete receipts and procedures for disputing charges if receipts are not provided.
- Offered guidance on documenting communications with Mercado Pago for legal claims, using screenshots, emails, and other [[documentation]] methods.
- Analyzed the risks and benefits of canceling payments via credit cards versus requesting receipts from Mercado Pago, with legal implications and claim management recommendations.

### Achievements
Completed the development of comprehensive guides and procedures for handling financial transaction disputes, providing clear steps and [[documentation]] requirements.
