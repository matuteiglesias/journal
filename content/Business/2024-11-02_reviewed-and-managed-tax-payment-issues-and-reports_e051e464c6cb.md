## Description

### Session Goal
The session aimed to review and manage issues related to tax payments, specifically focusing on the Impuesto Inmobiliario ABL, and to prepare related reports for internal and external stakeholders.

- Conducted detailed analysis of tax tickets related to the Impuesto Inmobiliario ABL, identifying errors and recommending process improvements.
