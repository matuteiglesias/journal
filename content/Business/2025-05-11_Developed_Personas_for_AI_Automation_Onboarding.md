**Session Goal:**
The primary goal of this session was to develop structured personas for [[AI]] automation onboarding, focusing on creating a comprehensive framework to guide the onboarding process for a RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) tool ecosystem.

**Key Activities:**
- Outlined primary personas for onboarding related to a RAG tool ecosystem, detailing their needs, value propositions, and suggested onboarding assets.
- Developed a structured approach to personas for [[AI]] automation, including profiles, needs, resources, and onboarding strategies for four distinct personas: [[AI]] [[Automation]] Engineer, Non-Technical Researcher, Indie Hacker, and End-User Client.
- Explored a trail-based onboarding publishing system using existing GPT logs to generate structured, audience-specific documentation.
- Designed a knowledge-grounded publishing engine to create persona-targeted documentation using past logs.

**Achievements:**
- Successfully mapped out primary personas and their onboarding needs for the RAG tool ecosystem.
- Developed a comprehensive framework for persona development in [[AI]] automation, including specific onboarding strategies.
- Conceptualized a trail-based onboarding publishing system and a knowledge-grounded publishing engine.
