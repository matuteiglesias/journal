### Session Goal
The session aimed to develop and refine strategies for managing family finances in an inflationary context, particularly focusing on optimizing expenses and improving financial sustainability.

### Key Activities
- **Guidance on Expense [[Optimization]]:** Explored strategies to optimize family expenses in an inflationary Argentine environment, including prioritizing payments, managing debts, and handling tenant contributions.
- **Proposal for Financial Reporting:** Developed a structured proposal for a family financial report section, highlighting its definition, key functions, and its relationship with periodic reports.
- **Financial Report Interpretation:** Provided instructions on interpreting family financial reports, breaking down balance readings, identifying assets and liabilities, and recognizing financial warning signs.
