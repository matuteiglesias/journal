### Session Goal
The session aimed to develop and refine marketing strategies for advertising local commercial rentals in Greater Buenos Aires (GBA), focusing on enhancing visibility and lead generation.

### Key Activities
- **Launch Plan Development**: Created a structured launch plan detailing strategies for advertising local commercial rentals in GBA. This included identifying top portals for visibility, setting photo guidelines, crafting compelling titles and descriptions, and employing multichannel approaches.
- **[[SEO]] [[Optimization]]**: Proposed [[SEO]]-friendly title suggestions for real estate listings to improve searchability and click-through rates. Discussed the importance of title length and keyword strategy.
- **Draft Diagnostics**: Conducted a diagnostic analysis of real estate listing drafts, highlighting key checkpoints and providing suggestions for improvement.
- **SERP Analysis**: Analyzed SERP snippets and provided instructions for capturing key elements for keyword analysis and communication tone.
- **Critical Review**: Critiqued SERP snippets and draft descriptions, identifying shortcomings and potential financial losses, and suggested refinements.
- **Draft Review**: Reviewed property listing drafts, identified issues such as inconsistent measurements and vague descriptions, and provided a checklist for finalization.

### Achievements
- Developed a comprehensive marketing strategy for GBA commercial rentals.
- Enhanced [[SEO]] strategies for real estate listings.
- Improved draft quality through diagnostics and reviews.
