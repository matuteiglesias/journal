### Session Goal
The session aimed to conceptualize and structure a professional dossier to enhance job applications by integrating various professional roles and crafting a coherent narrative.

### Key Activities
- **Creation of a Professional Dossier Template:** Explored the strategic advantages of a professional dossier that complements a CV and cover letter, providing a structured format for presentation.
- **[[Integration]] of Professional Axes:** Discussed the integration of five professional axes—Researcher, Engineer, GIS Expert, Consultant, and Communicator—to reinforce the candidate's profile.
- **Structuring a Coherent Dossier:** Developed a structured approach to integrate the roles into a coherent narrative, emphasizing the importance of internal logic.
- **Designing a Researcher Section:** Outlined a structured approach for the 'Researcher' section, including framing and curated research artifacts.
- **Next Steps for Researcher Section:** Identified actionable steps for polishing and finalizing the 'Researcher' section, including formatting and adding visuals.
- **Engineering Knowledge Pipelines:** Detailed the content for the 'Engineering Knowledge Pipelines' section, focusing on engineering practices and tools.

### Achievements

- Developed a coherent narrative structure integrating multiple professional roles.
- Outlined detailed sections for 'Researcher' and 'Engineering Knowledge Pipelines.'
