## 📅 2025-07-30 — Session: Developed professional dossier for job applications

**🕒 22:45–23:10**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Dossier, Job Application, Professional Development, Career Advice, Narrative Structure  
**📂 Project**: Business  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


### Session Goal
The session aimed to explore the development of a professional dossier to enhance job applications by complementing traditional CVs and cover letters.

### Key Activities
- Discussed the concept of a professional dossier that showcases targeted, curated demonstrations of expertise, emphasizing its strategic advantages.
- Explored the integration of five professional axes—Researcher, Engineer, GIS Expert, Consultant, and Communicator—into a candidate profile to reinforce qualifications and experience.
- Outlined a structured approach to creating a coherent narrative in a professional dossier, integrating various professional roles into a unified persona.

### Achievements
- Clarified the strategic advantages of a professional dossier in job applications.
- Developed a coherent integration strategy for multiple professional roles in a candidate profile.
- Established a structured approach for presenting a unified professional persona.

### Pending Tasks
- Further refine the narrative structure of the professional dossier to ensure clarity and coherence.
- Test the effectiveness of the professional dossier in actual job applications to gather feedback and make necessary adjustments.
