## 📅 2025-08-06 — Session: Analyzed Recruitment Feedback and Communication Etiquette

**🕒 20:50–21:00**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Recruitment, Feedback, Communication, Professionalism, Data Science  
**📂 Project**: Business  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


### Session Goal:
The session aimed to analyze recruitment feedback and explore communication etiquette for professional interactions.

### Key Activities:
- **Feedback Analysis:** Evaluated the feedback received from recruitment processes, identifying two main interpretations: a real mismatch of skills versus job requirements or a standard euphemism used by consultancies.
- **[[Communication]] Etiquette:** Suggested a more courteous and concise response template to maintain professional relationships and keep doors open for future opportunities.

### Achievements:
- Clarified the potential reasons behind recruitment feedback, providing strategic insights on how to interpret and respond to such feedback.
- Developed a template for professional communication, enhancing the ability to respond effectively and maintain positive connections.

### Pending Tasks:
- Further exploration of personalized feedback handling strategies to improve future job applications.
