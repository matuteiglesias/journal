### Session Goal
The goal of this session was to develop comprehensive CV templates for full-stack engineers, focusing on both backend and frontend skills, while ensuring clarity and honesty in presentation.

### Key Activities
- **Roadmap Creation**: Outlined a structured approach to crafting a full-stack engineer CV, emphasizing skills in [[Python]], React, Next.js, and DevOps.

- **Feedback and Refinement**: Provided detailed feedback on multiple CVs, identifying strengths and areas for improvement, and offering recommendations for consistency and clarity.
