## 📅 2025-08-06 — Session: Developed Tailored CV Templates for Role Families

**🕒 16:10–16:30**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Cv Optimization, Job Search, Data Engineering, Career Development  
**📂 Project**: Business  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


### Session Goal
The session aimed to develop a strategy for clustering job openings into six distinct role families, with the purpose of creating tailored CV templates. This would enhance keyword alignment and showcase relevant skills for each role type, particularly in the fields of data engineering, [[AI]]/ML, data science, and consultative roles.

### Key Activities
- Outlined a strategy for clustering job openings into six role families.
- Provided a detailed critique and actionable suggestions for rewriting CVs to align with Data Engineering roles.
- Developed a comprehensive roadmap for transforming CVs and LinkedIn profiles to better align with data engineering roles, emphasizing relevant skills, experiences, and keywords.

### Achievements
- Successfully created a strategy to cluster job openings, facilitating the creation of tailored CV templates.
- Offered actionable insights for optimizing CVs and LinkedIn profiles for data engineering roles.

### Pending Tasks
- Implement the developed strategy to create actual CV templates for each identified role family.
- Further refine the CV and LinkedIn profile optimization process based on recruiter feedback.
