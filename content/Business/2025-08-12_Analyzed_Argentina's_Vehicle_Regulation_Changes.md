**🕒 17:15–17:30**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Argentina, Cédula Azul, DNU 70/2023, Traffic Regulations, Legal Strategy  
**📂 Project**: Business  



### Session Goal
The session aimed to explore and understand recent changes in Argentina's vehicle regulations, focusing on the Cédula Azul and related legal frameworks.

### Key Activities
- Conducted search queries on Argentina's Cédula Azul regulations and recent decrees affecting driving authorizations for third parties.
- Investigated DNU 70/2023 and its implications on the motor vehicle registry, particularly articles 361 and 362.
- Reflected on the changes in the vehicular identification card system, including the shift to a digital system via the Mi Argentina app.
- Analyzed traffic fine adjudication in CABA, focusing on the absence of the Cédula Azul and its legal implications.
- Developed a structured approach for filing a defense with the Controlador de Faltas in Av. Patricios, including applicable legal frameworks and evidence strategies.
- Prepared strategic questions for a legal consultation regarding traffic infractions.
- Outlined legal strategies for judicial appeals in CABA if a case is rejected by the Controlador de Faltas.

### Achievements
- Clarified the impact of recent legal changes on vehicle identification and traffic regulations in Argentina.
- Developed comprehensive strategies for legal defense and appeals in traffic-related matters.
