### Session Goal:
The session aimed to develop and refine frameworks for economic and forensic analysis, focusing on variance decomposition, covariance analysis, and accounting methodologies.

### Key Activities:
1. **Modeling Sector Time Series:** Implemented a single-factor fit to model sector time series, decomposing aggregate variance into sectoral covariances. Provided options for LaTeX derivations and NumPy snippets for practical implementation.
2. **Covariance Analysis:** Explored foundational principles of separating comovement and idiosyncrasy in covariance analysis, emphasizing econometric choices and potential pitfalls.
3. **Financial Analysis Reflection:** Discussed the distinction between accounting and modeling, presenting methods for accounting aggregate volatility and clarifying misconceptions.
4. **Economic Accounting Framework:** Outlined a structured approach using Shapley attribution and projection methods to analyze economic data, addressing counterpoints and providing implementation guidance.
5. **Forensic Accounting Techniques:** Developed a workflow for forensic accounting, focusing on data partitioning, covariance calculation, and defensibility in reporting.
