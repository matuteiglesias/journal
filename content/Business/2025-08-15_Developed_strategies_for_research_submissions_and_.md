The session aimed to establish a structured approach for submitting research papers weekly and to design an ingestion stack for economic research papers.

### Key Activities
- **Research Submission [[Strategy]]**: Developed a workflow for submitting one research paper per week, including roles, quality checks, and a selection process for candidate papers.
- **Ingestion Stack Design**: Planned a comprehensive ingestion stack for economic research papers, covering data sources, polling strategies, normalization, enrichment, and ranking.
- **Strategic Foresight**: Explored strategic foresight in research operations, proposing a checklist for efficient execution of a process called 'King's Hour'.
