### Session Goal
The session aimed to develop frameworks for collaborative research and outreach strategies by analyzing researchers' styles and integrating them with personal methodologies.

### Key Activities
- **Intelligence Mapping and Outreach [[Strategy]]**: Developed a structured approach to analyze a researcher's style and integrate it into a collaborative proposal. This involved learning the researcher's style, cross-referencing with personal methodologies, and preparing outreach drafts.
