**Session Goal:**
The session aimed to explore and develop strategies for payment processing solutions tailored for the Argentinian market, particularly focusing on preparing an agency for sale and understanding payment options for Argentinian clients dealing with a European company.

1. Reviewed and utilized a comprehensive checklist for preparing an agency for sale, including steps from landing page creation to Stripe integration and analytics systems.
