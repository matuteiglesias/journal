### Session Goal
The session aimed to analyze and enhance strategies for academic and community interventions, focusing on improving communication and strategic approaches in discussions involving [[AI]] and institutional frameworks.

### Key Activities
- **Discussion Analysis**: Reviewed the Vibecoding workshop, focusing on criticisms of [[AI]]-assisted programming and the BMAD method's structured approach.
