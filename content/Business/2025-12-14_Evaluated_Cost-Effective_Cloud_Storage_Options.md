## 📅 2025-12-14 — Session: Evaluated Cost-Effective Cloud Storage Options

**🕒 16:10–19:00**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Cloud Storage, Cost-Effective, Encryption, Backup, Google Drive  
**📂 Project**: Business  



**Session Goal:**
The goal of this session was to explore and evaluate cost-effective cloud storage solutions as alternatives to Google Storage for capacities ranging from 100 GB to 500 GB.

**Key Activities:**
- Researched and documented various affordable cloud storage services such as IDrive, Wasabi, Backblaze, pCloud, MEGA, and Sync, focusing on pricing and performance.
- Compared security features of cloud storage services including encryption, compliance, and zero-knowledge security.
- Recommended pCloud with client-side encryption and Backblaze B2 for users with different security needs.
- Investigated Google Storage options, including Google One, Google Workspace, and Google Cloud Storage, focusing on pricing, features, and encryption methods.
- Developed a backup [[automation]] script using bash and rclone for Google Drive, including safety checks and encryption steps.

**Achievements:**
- Identified and compared multiple cost-effective cloud storage solutions with detailed insights into their security features.
- Provided actionable recommendations for cloud storage based on security and cost considerations.
- Created a robust backup script for automating data backup to Google Drive.

**Pending Tasks:**
- Further analysis on the [[integration]] of recommended cloud storage solutions into existing workflows.
- Testing and validation of the backup [[automation]] script in different environments.
