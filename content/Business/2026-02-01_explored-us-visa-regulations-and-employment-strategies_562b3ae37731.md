## Description

### Session Goal:
The session aimed to explore and understand U.S. visa regulations, particularly focusing on employment restrictions and potential pathways for work authorization for individuals on visitor visas.

### Key Activities:
- Conducted search queries on various aspects of U.S. visa regulations, including visitor visa employment restrictions, change of status processes, and eligibility criteria for extraordinary ability and National Interest Waiver visas.
- Investigated specific visa categories such as B-2, H-1B, E-2, and O-1A, focusing on employment restrictions and treaty information relevant to Argentina.
- Summarized findings regarding the legal constraints on employment for B-1 and B-2 visa holders, emphasizing that these visas do not permit work.
- Outlined strategies for obtaining work authorization while on a visitor visa, including employer-sponsored visas and other pathways like O-1A, L-1, and EB-2 NIW.

### Achievements:
- Gained insights into the restrictions and possibilities for employment under different U.S. visa categories.
- Clarified the legal separation between visitor activities and those requiring work authorization.

### Pending Tasks:

- Explore additional pathways for individuals with extraordinary abilities or national interest waivers.
