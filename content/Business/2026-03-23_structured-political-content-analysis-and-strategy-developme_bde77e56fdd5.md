## Description

### Session Goal
The session aimed to develop a structured approach for analyzing and organizing political content, focusing on enhancing public communication and data governance strategies in Argentina.

### Key Activities
- **Criteria Development**: Established criteria for constructing a personal political corpus to reflect a public voice in Argentine political discourse.
- **Material Structuring**: Proposed a framework to categorize content into public voice, concepts, and technopolitical authority.
- **Content Evaluation**: Analyzed and selected relevant content from various batches (March, August, October, November, December 2023, and January, February 2024) focusing on themes like Peronism, economic policies, poverty, and data governance.
- **Strategic Analysis**: Evaluated materials for data policy subcommittees and analyzed electoral and legislative materials to propose strategic political insights.

### Achievements
- Developed a comprehensive framework for categorizing political content.
- Identified key themes and materials for strategic communication and governance.
- Proposed strategic insights for enhancing institutional authority and public engagement in political discourse.
