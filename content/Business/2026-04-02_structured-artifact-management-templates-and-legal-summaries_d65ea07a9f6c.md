## Description

## Session Goal
Build reusable, low-bureaucracy templates for managing artifacts across governance, [[accounting]], legal, and family/conflict contexts.

## Key Activities
- Proposed a **16-column sheet structure** for artifact inventory management, aimed at capturing what each artifact is, why it matters, its current state, and next actions without adding unnecessary process overhead.
- Reviewed an **artifact management overview** for governance/[[strategy]], framing artifacts as a prioritized inventory that supports decision-making and follow-up.
- Drafted a **meeting agenda and guidelines package** for family discussions, including opening scripts, decision checklists, and follow-up templates to improve communication and execution.
- Created an **artifact management table for [[accounting]]**, organizing evidence and [[documentation]] by type, function, and next steps.
- Outlined **legal artifact checklists** tied to CCyC / legal materials, focusing on [[documentation]], questions, and action prompts for legal professionals.
- Assembled **conflict timeline and stakeholder summaries** to map events, economic flows, and stakeholder dynamics into a structured case-management view.
- Produced an **artifact overview for legal stakeholders** with executive summaries, matrices, and guides to help lawyers and stakeholders understand the case and prepare objections.

## Achievements
- Established a coherent pattern for turning messy operational, legal, and family information into structured artifact inventories.
- Clarified the intent of the system: support governance, [[accounting]], and legal coordination with concise templates that enable action tracking and prioritization.
- Identified a reusable [[documentation]] approach that can be applied across multiple domains with minimal friction.

## Pending Tasks
