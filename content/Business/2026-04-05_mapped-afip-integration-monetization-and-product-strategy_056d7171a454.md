Explore how to monetize AFIP [[integration]] expertise by identifying structural advantages, viable business models, and product opportunities in Argentina’s compliance and electronic invoicing ecosystem.

- Reviewed strategic framing for monetizing AFIP [[integration]] knowledge as a business asset rather than a one-off technical skill.
