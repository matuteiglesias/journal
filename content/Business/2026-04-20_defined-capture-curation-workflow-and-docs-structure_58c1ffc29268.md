## Description

## Session Goal
Clarify the product and [[documentation]] [[architecture]] for a capture-to-artifact system, with emphasis on the post-capture curation layer and the downstream governance needed to keep the system operational and scalable.

## Key Activities
- Compared a generic "[[AI]] product" framing against **vertical microproducts** that can be monetized quickly, and ranked candidate ideas using criteria like willingness to pay, incumbents, and speed to first sale.
- Refined the core product thesis: the real value is not raw capture, but the **post-capture curation layer** that decides what gets promoted, merged, discarded, actioned, or reused.
- Proposed a **dual-axis lifecycle model** separating retention state from [[workflow]] state to avoid inbox bloat and preserve a clean artifact lifecycle.
- Designed a **queue-based triage model** with promotion gates, merge rules, selective decay, and human/system roles to keep review lightweight but disciplined.
- Drafted [[documentation]] structure changes for [[Docusaurus]], including new pages for **Capture Items** and **Curation & Review**, plus sidebar configuration to make the docs navigable and source-of-truth aligned.

## Achievements
- Established a clearer product wedge: the system should be validated manually first around curation and reuse, rather than polished capture UI.
- Formalized the downstream governance concept with explicit lifecycle states and review actions.
- Identified [[documentation]] additions needed to bridge raw capture and durable artifacts without expanding ontology unnecessarily.
- Produced ready-to-use [[documentation]] structure guidance, including sidebar organization for the MAL docs site.

## Pending Tasks
- Validate the curation [[workflow]] manually with a narrow wedge use case before building a full app.
- Turn the lifecycle and review rules into a minimal schema and operational checklist.
- Decide which vertical microproduct idea has the strongest path to an early paid pilot or first sale.
- Implement or update the [[Docusaurus]] pages and sidebar to reflect the new capture governance model.
