## Session Goal
Design a tightly bounded V1 [[architecture]] for an economic claim-to-brief pipeline, with a focus on controlled brief generation rather than a full publishing system.

- Reviewed an audit of the top 50 economic series to identify duplicates, methodological variants, and indicator clusters.

- Captured a practical communication principle: prioritize simple, actionable guidance over perfection in urgent contexts.
