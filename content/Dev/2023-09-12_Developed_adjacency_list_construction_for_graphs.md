### Session Goal
The session aimed to explore and demonstrate the construction of adjacency lists for graph representations, focusing on their time complexity and comparing them with other data structures like hash tables and adjacency matrices.

### Key Activities
- Detailed exploration of the process for constructing adjacency lists in graphs.
- Comparison of adjacency lists with hash tables and adjacency matrices in terms of time complexity.
- Demonstration of the construction process, providing a rigorous analysis of its advantages and limitations.

### Achievements
- Successfully detailed the construction method for adjacency lists, providing insights into their efficiency and practical applications.
- Clarified the differences in complexity and use cases between adjacency lists, hash tables, and adjacency matrices.
