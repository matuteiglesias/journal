### Session Goal
The goal of this session was to explore and compare various [[AI]] prompt systems, focusing on tools from Microsoft and other major tech companies, to understand their features, advantages, and potential for [[integration]].

### Key Activities
- Conducted a comparative analysis of Microsoft's [[AI]] tools, including Microsoft Guidance, FlowML/[[PromptFlow]], and AutoGen, highlighting their alignment with composable prompt systems.

- Explored the features of PromptFoo and Prompt Runner, identifying their roles in prompt testing and execution, respectively.
