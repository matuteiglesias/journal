### Session Goal
The session aimed to understand and explore the integration of OpenTelemetry (OTel) with Azure's `azure-core-tracing-opentelemetry` for enhancing application monitoring and tracing capabilities.

### Key Activities
- Reviewed the significance of OpenTelemetry and Azure tracing in monitoring application operations, particularly in the context of [[AI]] orchestration and job automation.
- Explored strategies for implementing OpenTelemetry-based tracing without relying on Azure SDK telemetry, focusing on necessary packages, setup instructions, and potential future export options.

### Achievements
- Gained insights into the use of OpenTelemetry for tracking application performance and failures, which is crucial for [[AI]] orchestration.
- Developed a strategy for setting up tracing without the Azure SDK, providing flexibility in monitoring solutions.
