### Session Goal
The goal of this session was to evaluate and analyze the performance of various classifiers used for predicting different income categories, including general income (`INGRESO`), non-labor income (`INGRESO_NLB`), and pension income (`INGRESO_JUB`). Additionally, the session aimed to address issues related to model collapse due to class imbalance.

### Key Activities
- Conducted a detailed evaluation of the `INGRESO` classifier, including performance metrics, confusion analysis, and potential causes for misclassification.
- Analyzed the `INGRESO_NLB` classifier's strengths and weaknesses, providing tactical recommendations for improvement.
- Reviewed the `INGRESO_JUB` classifier's effectiveness, focusing on precision and recall, and suggested refinements for accuracy improvement.
- Investigated the impact of class imbalance on classifier performance, specifically addressing issues with class 1 predictions and recommending adjustments for better recall.

### Achievements
- Completed a comprehensive performance review of multiple classifiers, identifying key areas for improvement and refinement.
- Developed actionable recommendations for model adjustments and data strategies to enhance classifier performance under class imbalance conditions.
