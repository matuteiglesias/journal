### Session Goal
The session aimed to explore and develop methods for transforming text chunks into structured hierarchies and converting chat/markdown corpora into functional wikis.

### Key Activities
- Explored techniques for creating navigable hierarchies from text chunks using algorithms like k-NN with MST, agglomerative clustering, and Topic-Tree Transformers.
- Reviewed a compact field-guide for transforming chat or markdown corpora into wikis, focusing on fragmentation, embedding, clustering, and regeneration processes.
- Examined hierarchy-aware wrappers for document management, such as LlamaIndex, LangChain, RAPTOR, and TMAP, to automate document hierarchy management.

### Achievements
- Gained insights into practical methods and [[Python]] code snippets for text processing and hierarchical structuring.
- Acquired actionable tactics and resources for wiki generation from large corpora.
- Identified various document management wrappers and SDKs, understanding their functionalities, use cases, and trade-offs.
