### Session Goal
The session aimed to explore and enhance techniques for saving, loading, and improving hierarchical clustering in [[Python]] using libraries such as `scipy` and `numpy`.

### Key Activities

- Discussed the use of `fcluster` for threshold-based segmentation and explored its limitations.
- Proposed alternative methods for achieving meaningful hierarchical cluster sizes, including `maxclust`, distance percentiles, HDBSCAN, and leaf ordering.
- Outlined strategies for clustering hierarchical data, emphasizing the importance of selecting appropriate thresholds and techniques.
