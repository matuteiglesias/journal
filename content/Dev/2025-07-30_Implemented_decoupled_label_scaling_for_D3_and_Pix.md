**Session Goal:**
The session aimed to address the issue of label scaling during zoom operations in D3 and Pixi.js graphs by decoupling the scaling of labels from nodes.

**Key Activities:**
- Explored a solution to prevent text labels from scaling when zooming in a D3 force-graph by separating the rendering of nodes and labels into distinct groups.
- Provided code snippets and instructions for handling zoom scaling issues in Pixi.js, specifically addressing the problem of label shrinking during zoom.

**Achievements:**
- Successfully outlined and implemented solutions for decoupling label scaling from node scaling in both D3 and Pixi.js environments.
