### Session Goal
The session aimed to address technical challenges related to UI component z-index conflicts in Obsidian and to optimize node density in force-directed graph visualizations. Additionally, an analysis of hub-and-spoke network structures in knowledge graphs was conducted.

### Key Activities

- **Network Structure Analysis**: Analyzed the hub-and-spoke architecture in knowledge graphs, identifying strengths in intra-cluster coherence and proposing strategies to enhance inter-cluster connections through bridging nodes and cross-topic links.
