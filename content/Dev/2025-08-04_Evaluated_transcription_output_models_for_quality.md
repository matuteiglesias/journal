## 📅 2025-08-04 — Session: Evaluated transcription output models for quality

**🕒 13:55–14:00**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Quality Assessment, Transcription, Diarization, Text Analysis  
**📂 Project**: Dev  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


### Session Goal
The session aimed to evaluate the quality of two output models in the domain of speech recognition, focusing on transcription and diarization.

### Key Activities
- Conducted a quality assessment of two output models, comparing the web-app output with a diarized output.
- Evaluated the models based on three axes: accuracy of content, segmentation & readability, and coverage & completeness.
- Highlighted strengths and weaknesses in each category, providing a detailed comparison.
- Analyzed the textual accuracy, segmentation, readability, and speaker attribution of the transcription outputs.

### Achievements
- Successfully identified the advantages of a diarization-driven small-model run over a web app transcript.
- Clarified the strengths and weaknesses of each transcription model in terms of accuracy, segmentation, and readability.

### Pending Tasks
- Further analysis may be required to refine the diarization model for even better accuracy and segmentation.
