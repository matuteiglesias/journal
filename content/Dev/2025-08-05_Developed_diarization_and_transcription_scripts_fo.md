### Session Goal
The session aimed to develop and refine scripts for the diarization and transcription of audio files using the Whisper model.

### Key Activities
- Developed [[Python]] scripts to process `.wav` audio files and corresponding `.rttm` files, applying diarization and transcription using the Whisper model.
- Created scripts to convert audio files from formats like `.wav`, `.m4a`, and `.webm` to `.wav` format, followed by diarization and transcription, generating speaker-labeled text files with timestamps.
- Addressed issues related to missing `.rttm` files necessary for diarization by providing a guide to verify paths and ensure the correct execution of the diarization script.

### Achievements
- Successfully implemented a workflow for audio diarization and transcription, including format conversion and error resolution related to `.rttm` files.
