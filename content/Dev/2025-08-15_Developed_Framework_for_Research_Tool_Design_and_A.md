## 📅 2025-08-15 — Session: Developed Framework for Research Tool Design and Automation

**🕒 11:00–12:35**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Research Tools, Automation, Embedding, Document Retrieval, Scaling  
**📂 Project**: Dev  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


### Session Goal
The session aimed to design and strategize the development of various research tools and frameworks to enhance academic processes, focusing on automation, embedding, and document retrieval.

### Key Activities
- **Ingestion Stack for Economic Papers**: Developed a comprehensive plan for creating an ingestion stack, including sources, polling strategies, normalization, enrichment, and ranking.
- **Strategic Foresight in Research Operations**: Explored strategic foresight concepts and created a checklist for efficient research execution.
- **Research Foresight Framework**: Outlined a workflow for processing academic papers to generate actionable insights.
- **Multi-View Embedding [[Pipeline]]**: Planned a multi-view embedding pipeline for research snippet analysis.
- **Citation Impact Strategies**: Discussed strategies to enhance citation impact by balancing references and employing strategic citations.
- **Multi-Scale Literature Browser**: Conceptualized a literature browser with a focus on UI/UX and data pipeline.
- **Research Tool Design**: Addressed challenges in research tool development, focusing on stability, trustworthy summaries, and speed.
- **Scientific Articles Estimation**: Estimated the number of non-health scientific articles and their storage implications.
- **Embedding Cost and Time Analysis**: Analyzed costs and time for embedding 40 million abstracts using various models.
- **Document Retrieval System Design**: Designed a high-performance document retrieval system architecture.
- **Full-Stack Development with Search Infrastructure**: Reflected on the complexities of integrating information retrieval and data infrastructure.
- **Progressive Scaling [[Strategy]]**: Developed a scaling strategy for an abstract navigation app.

### Achievements
- Created detailed frameworks and execution plans for various research tools and processes.
- Developed strategic insights for enhancing research impact and efficiency.
