### Session Goal
The session aimed to develop a structured strategy for weekly paper submissions and explore API documentation for academic research integration.

### Key Activities
- **Weekly Paper Submission [[Strategy]]**: Developed a structured approach for submitting one paper per week, focusing on roles, quality gates, and submission cadence to ensure efficiency and quality.
- **API [[Documentation]] Search**: Conducted search queries for various academic paper APIs, including arXiv, SSRN, NBER, RePEc, OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, and Crossref, to gather documentation and RSS feed parameters.
- **Economic Research RSS Feeds**: Searched for RSS feeds related to economic research papers from CEPR, IMF, BIS, and ECB.
- **Ingestion Stack Design**: Outlined a comprehensive ingestion stack for economic research papers, detailing sources, polling plans, normalization processes, enrichment strategies, ranking methodologies, and stub page generation.
- **Strategic Foresight in Research Operations**: Explored the concept of strategic foresight in research operations, detailing its components and suggesting potential names for this function.

### Achievements
- Established a clear weekly paper submission strategy.
- Gathered essential API documentation and RSS feed parameters for various academic research APIs.
- Designed a detailed ingestion stack for economic research papers.
