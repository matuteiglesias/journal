## 📅 2025-08-15 — Session: Developed strategies for embedding and retrieval systems

**🕒 12:00–12:15**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Embedding, Retrieval System, Scientific Articles, Cost Analysis, System Design  
**📂 Project**: Dev  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


### Session Goal
The session aimed to estimate the volume of non-health/biomedical scientific articles, analyze cost and time for embedding 40 million abstracts, and design a high-performance document retrieval system.

### Key Activities
1. **Estimation of Non-Health/Bio Scientific Articles**: Provided a detailed estimation of the number of scientific and engineering articles published globally, excluding health and biomedical fields. This included a decade-wise breakdown and implications for data storage and operationalization.

2. **Cost and Time Analysis for Embedding 40M Abstracts**: Conducted a detailed breakdown of costs and time estimates for processing 40 million abstracts using various embedding models and providers. The analysis included pricing, throughput rates, and practical recommendations for efficient embedding strategies.

3. **Designing a High-Performance Document Retrieval System**: Outlined the architecture and design goals for an efficient document retrieval system capable of handling 40 million abstracts with fast filtering and scrolling capabilities. The design detailed core data structures, backend architecture, frontend considerations, capacity planning, and phased delivery for implementation.

### Achievements
- Clarified the global volume of non-health/biomedical scientific articles and their data implications.
- Developed a comprehensive cost and time model for embedding large datasets.
- Designed a robust framework for a document retrieval system to efficiently manage and access large datasets.

### Pending Tasks
- Finalize the selection of embedding models and providers based on cost and performance.
- Begin implementation of the document retrieval system as per the outlined design.
