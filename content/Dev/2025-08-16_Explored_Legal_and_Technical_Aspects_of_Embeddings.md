### Session Goal
The session aimed to explore the legal and technical considerations involved in using embeddings and quotes in [[AI]] applications, with a focus on copyright risks and fair use principles. Additionally, it sought to understand the tension between knowledge as a public good versus a proprietary asset, particularly in the context of academic publishing and open access.

### Key Activities
- **Legal and Technical Nuances**: Discussed strategies for embedding abstracts while minimizing legal exposure, and explored alternative approaches for handling text data.
- **Knowledge as Public Good**: Analyzed the cultural shift towards open access and the role of abstracts in academic publishing.
- **Coverage-First Approach**: Developed a structured plan for maximizing the coverage of research abstracts, ensuring legal compliance and clear provenance.

### Achievements
- Proposed a strategy for embedding abstracts with minimized legal risks.
- Gained insights into the cultural shift towards open access in academic publishing.
- Outlined a comprehensive plan for abstract retrieval and data processing, focusing on legal compliance.
