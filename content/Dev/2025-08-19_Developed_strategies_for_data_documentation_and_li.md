## 📅 2025-08-19 — Session: Developed strategies for data documentation and literature harvesting

**🕒 09:00–09:30**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Data Documentation, Literature Harvesting, Poverty Data, Research Insights, Openalex, Crossref  
**📂 Project**: Dev  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


### Session Goal
The session aimed to develop structured strategies for authoring data documentation and designing a targeted literature harvester.

### Key Activities
1. **Data [[Documentation]] [[Strategy]]**: A structured approach was outlined for authoring data documentation, focusing on improving structure, defining author-level contracts, providing page templates, and mapping clusters to sections for better traceability.
2. **Research Insights**: Reviewed recent advancements in representation learning, generative modeling, and econometric methods relevant to poverty data analysis, providing actionable insights for students and researchers.
3. **Literature Harvester Design**: Designed an operational strategy for a literature harvester to compile a corpus of approximately 1,000 academic papers, utilizing OpenAlex and Crossref for data enrichment and retrieval.

### Achievements
- Established a clear framework for data documentation that enhances quality control and traceability.
- Gained insights into advanced data analysis techniques applicable to poverty measurement.
- Developed a comprehensive plan for a literature harvester to improve academic research efficiency.

### Pending Tasks
- Implementation of the literature harvester design to begin data retrieval and enrichment.
- Further exploration of the application of recent research insights in practical poverty data projects.
