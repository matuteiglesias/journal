## 📅 2025-08-19 — Session: Integrated SPG and Deep Learning Framework Development

**🕒 13:20–13:30**  
**🏷️ Labels**: SPG, Deep Learning, Poverty Analysis, Generative Models, Microsimulation

### Session Goal
The session aimed to develop a framework that integrates classical synthetic population generation (SPG) methods with modern deep learning techniques, focusing on identifying overlaps and gaps in current research and proposing structured approaches for future investigations.

### Key Activities
1. **Framework Development**: Outlined a framework for integrating SPG methods with deep learning techniques such as autoencoders and contrastive learning, particularly in the context of poverty research.
2. **SERP Analysis**: Conducted an analysis of search engine results pages (SERP) to identify key papers and contributions in the field of generative methods for survey and census household data, including the use of CTGAN and survey synthesis.
3. **Methodology Mapping**: Mapped the integration of traditional microsimulation models with GAN and autoencoder approaches in poverty measurement, highlighting key literature and methodologies.

### Achievements
- Developed a comprehensive framework for SPG and deep learning integration.
- Identified key papers and contributions in generative methods for household data.
- Mapped methodologies for integrating microsimulation and GAN techniques in poverty analysis.

### Pending Tasks

- Validation and testing of the proposed framework in real-world scenarios to assess its effectiveness.
