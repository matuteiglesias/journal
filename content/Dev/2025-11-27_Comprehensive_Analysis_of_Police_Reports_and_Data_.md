## 📅 2025-11-27 — Session: Comprehensive Analysis of Police Reports and Data Strategy

**🕒 18:40–19:15**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Police Report Analysis, Data Strategy, Criminal Analysis, P.U. 4810/2025, P.U. 5888/2025  
**📂 Project**: Dev  



### Session Goal
The session aimed to conduct a comprehensive analysis of specific police reports (P.U. 4810/2025 and P.U. 5888/2025) and to design a data [[strategy]] for criminal analysis.

### Key Activities
1. **Analysis of P.U. 4810/2025**: Evaluated police training and procedural discipline, identified omissions and procedural risks, and proposed corrections along with an operational checklist to enhance report quality.
2. **Critical Analysis of P.U. 5888/2025**: Conducted a detailed examination of police procedures, focusing on protocols involving minors, and provided a corrected version of the report with a compliance checklist.
3. **General Police Report Analysis**: Identified signs of professional discipline, omissions, procedural risks, and made recommendations to improve [[documentation]] and ensure evidentiary validity.
4. **Design of Semi-Structured Dataset for Criminal Analysis**: Proposed a detailed plan to convert P.U. data into a semi-structured dataset for use by the Subsecretaría de Planificación e Inteligencia Criminal, including data schema specifications and quality metrics.
5. **Two-Step [[Data Extraction]] [[Strategy]]**: Developed a phased [[data extraction]] approach using regex and heuristics for initial data collection, followed by [[AI]] and human review for data enrichment and normalization.

### Achievements
- Completed detailed analyses of police reports P.U. 4810/2025 and P.U. 5888/2025.
- Developed a comprehensive data [[strategy]] for criminal analysis, including dataset design and a two-step extraction process.

### Pending Tasks

- Validate the corrections and recommendations made for the police reports.
