## Description

### Session Goal:
The session aimed to compile and organize comprehensive guides for Brazilian citizens migrating to Argentina, focusing on legal, educational, and healthcare processes.

### Key Activities:
- Created a hierarchical list of retail items for a store in Gran Buenos Aires, categorized under general categories like beverages, food, and cleaning products.
- Detailed the requirements and steps for obtaining the Documento Nacional de Identidad (DNI) in Argentina, including special considerations for foreigners.
- Provided a guide for Brazilian citizens on obtaining temporary residency in Argentina, covering necessary [[documentation]] and costs.
- Outlined the procedure for same-sex marriage in Argentina for Brazilian citizens, with required [[documentation]].
- Compiled requirements for Brazilian nurses to work in Argentina, including title revalidation and accreditation with the Ministry of Health.
- Developed a detailed guide for the revalidation of nursing titles at the University of Buenos Aires (UBA), including administrative processes and exams.
- Integrated a comprehensive guide for Brazilian citizens on residency, DNI acquisition, and nursing title revalidation in Argentina.

### Achievements:
