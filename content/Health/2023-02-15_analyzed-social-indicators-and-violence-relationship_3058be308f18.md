## Description

### Session Goal
The session aimed to explore the relationship between violence and social indicators, focusing on research methodologies and statistical techniques to analyze this complex interaction.

### Key Activities
- **Research Summary**: Reviewed findings on how violence impacts economic, educational, and health outcomes, considering social inequality and contextual factors.
- **Methodological Planning**: Outlined key considerations for using Randomized Controlled Trials (RCT) and Propensity Score Matching (PSM) in social research.
- **Covariate Selection**: Discussed relevant covariates for studies on violence's impact on political and social indicators.
- **Confounding Variables**: Analyzed statistical methods for identifying confounding variables in violence studies, including stratification, regression analysis, and PSM.
- **Technical Guide**: Provided instructions for applying PSM in observational studies to control for confounding factors.

### Achievements
- Clarified the impact of violence on various social indicators and the role of social inequality.
- Established a framework for using RCT and PSM in social research.
- Identified relevant covariates and confounding variables for violence impact studies.
- Developed a step-by-step guide for implementing PSM in observational studies.
