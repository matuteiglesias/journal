## Description

**Session Goal:**
The session aimed to develop a comprehensive framework for nutritional tracking using a 'dosis-antidote' method and to perform clustering analysis on food items based on their macronutrient profiles.

**Key Activities:**
- Outlined a flexible dietary management approach using the 'dosis-antidote' method, focusing on long-term nutritional intake tracking.
- Provided generic naming conventions and nutritional estimates for chicken products to aid in dietary planning.
- Reflected on the nutritional database entries for chicken and pork, emphasizing the differences in nutritional content between raw and cooked states and the importance of correct measurements.
- Identified raw potato varieties and lean beef products suitable for nutritional tracking, highlighting lower calorie options and ideal fat-protein ratios.
- Executed a clustering analysis of food items using KMeans and StandardScaler, grouping them by macronutrient profiles and visualizing the clusters.

**Achievements:**
- Established a framework for flexible dietary management using the 'dosis-antidote' method.
- Clarified nutritional tracking details for chicken, pork, potatoes, and beef, enhancing the accuracy of dietary choices.
- Successfully clustered food items based on macronutrient profiles, providing insights into dietary planning and food selection.

**Pending Tasks:**
- Further refine the clustering analysis to include more diverse food items and explore additional clustering techniques.
- Implement the 'dosis-antidote' method in a practical dietary plan and monitor its effectiveness over time.
