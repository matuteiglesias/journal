### Session Goal
The objective of this session was to structure daily tasks into hourly blocks to enhance productivity and manage multiple responsibilities effectively over a 10 to 12 day period. Additionally, it aimed to explore strategies for overcoming procrastination and improving sleep discipline.

### Key Activities
- Developed a daily task structuring plan using hourly blocks to ensure balanced progress across various tasks.
- Explored strategies for overcoming procrastination, including time blocking, setting smaller goals, and implementing a reward system.
- Investigated the use of sleep aids such as melatonin and the importance of consulting healthcare providers before use.
- Discussed the implementation of alarms and a structured daily schedule to maintain discipline and optimize productivity.

### Achievements
- A comprehensive plan for daily task management was outlined, focusing on time management and productivity.
- Practical strategies for improving discipline and combating procrastination were identified.
- Guidelines for the optimal use of melatonin and sleep aids were reviewed.
