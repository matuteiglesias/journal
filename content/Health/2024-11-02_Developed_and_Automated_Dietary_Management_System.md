## 📅 2024-11-02 — Session: Developed and Automated Dietary Management System

**🕒 19:10–20:05**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Dietary Management, Recipe Optimization, Stock Control, Automation, Nutrition  
**📂 Project**: Health  



### Session Goal
The primary goal of this session was to develop and automate a dietary management system that optimizes recipes with new ingredients, manages dietary ingredient codes, evaluates balanced diets, and implements a stock control system.

### Key Activities
- **Recipe [[Optimization]]**: Explored methods to optimize recipes by incorporating new ingredients, providing detailed recipes for savory pancakes and cheesy tapioca bites.
- **Dietary Ingredient Management**: Outlined codes and placeholders for dietary ingredients, seeking user input for specific quantities.
- **Balanced Diet Evaluation**: Analyzed a balanced diet focusing on protein, fiber, vitamins, minerals, and areas for improvement such as cholesterol and saturated fats.
- **Stock Control System**: Planned and executed a stock control system using [[Python]] and Excel to manage food inventory, predict replenishment needs, and automate alerts.

### Achievements
- Successfully integrated new ingredients into existing recipes and provided storage tips.
- Developed a framework for dietary ingredient management and balanced diet evaluation.
- Designed and partially implemented a stock control system using [[Python]] and Excel.
