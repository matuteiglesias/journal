## 📅 2025-02-16 — Session: Development and Deployment of Nursing Shift Schedules

**🕒 20:00–21:55**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Nursing Schedules, 12X36 Model, Excel Templates, Pandas, Ilpis  
**📂 Project**: Health  



### Session Goal
The session aimed to develop and deploy various 12x36 work schedules, particularly focusing on nursing and healthcare settings, utilizing Excel templates and [[Pandas]] for data manipulation.

### Key Activities
- Defined the concept of 'Talle XL' in fashion, providing size equivalences.
- Developed and made available for download the 12x36 work schedule for 2025 in Portuguese and Excel formats.
- Created a nursing shift schedule in the 12x36 model, available for download, with options for customization.
- Explored different nursing schedule models, highlighting their pros and cons.
- Created a structured nursing shift schedule table using [[Pandas]], including a MultiIndex structure for data entry.
- Discussed the role of nurses in Long-Term Care Facilities for the Elderly (ILPIs), emphasizing management and direct care.

### Achievements
- Successfully created and deployed multiple work schedule templates for 2025, including those tailored for nursing.
- Developed a [[Pandas]] [[DataFrame]] structure to replicate nursing schedules, facilitating [[data management]].
- Provided insights into the role of nursing in ILPIs, enhancing understanding of healthcare management.
