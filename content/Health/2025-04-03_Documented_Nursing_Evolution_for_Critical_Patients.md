## 📅 2025-04-03 — Session: Documented Nursing Evolution for Critical Patients

**🕒 09:00–10:30**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Nursing, Critical Care, Patient Evolution, COPD, Cancer  
**📂 Project**: Health  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


### Session Goal:
The session aimed to document the nursing evolution of critical patients, focusing on those with severe health conditions such as COPD and esophageal cancer.

### Key Activities:
- Detailed nursing evolution records were created for a 97-year-old patient with critical COPD, including clinical aspects and nursing conduct.
- Instructions for nursing evolution of a similarly critical patient were documented, covering vital signs, nursing interventions, and communication with medical staff.
- A detailed report on the nursing evolution of an elderly patient was compiled, highlighting clinical status, care provided, and relevant observations.
- The clinical evolution of a male patient with esophageal cancer was documented, detailing his condition, nursing interventions, and an incident involving a fall.

### Achievements:
- Comprehensive nursing evolution records for critical patients were successfully documented, providing a clear overview of patient status and nursing interventions.
