## 📅 2025-06-11 — Session: Enhanced SOAP Templates for Post-Operative Care

**🕒 13:30–15:30**  
**🏷️ Labels**: SOAP, Tasy, Post-Operative, Clinical Documentation, Healthcare  
**📂 Project**: Health  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


### Session Goal
The session aimed to enhance the documentation of post-operative care using SOAP (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan) templates tailored for the Tasy electronic medical record system.

### Key Activities
- Reviewed and provided suggestions to improve documentation of post-operative evolution reports, focusing on pre-anesthetic checks, anesthesia type, bleeding, monitoring, and medications.
- Developed a reorganized SOAP note template for Tasy, including detailed observations for each section to improve traceability.
- Created structured SOAP notes for documenting post-operative care for vasectomy and tubal ligation procedures, ensuring clarity and conciseness in clinical documentation.

### Achievements
- Successfully developed and refined SOAP templates for various post-operative procedures, enhancing the quality and traceability of clinical documentation in Tasy.

### Pending Tasks
- Further review and testing of the new SOAP templates in real clinical settings to ensure effectiveness and usability.
