## Description

**Session Goal:** The session aimed to analyze and reflect on various clinical cases in the healthcare domain, focusing on risk classification, emergency response, and prioritization of medical care.

**Key Activities:**
1. **Risk Classification Protocol:** Corrected misconceptions about selecting severity descriptors, emphasizing choosing the highest severity in doubt.
2. **Blood Pressure Parameter Evaluation:** Reflected on alternative methods for assessing blood pressure parameters, identifying incorrect alternatives related to shock and treatment prioritization.
3. **Glasgow Coma Scale Analysis:** Analyzed a specific case to determine the Glasgow Coma Scale (GCS) score, resulting in a total of 7.
4. **Clinical Case Analyses:** Conducted detailed analyses of various clinical cases, including:
   - Elderly patient with cardiac symptoms indicating atypical acute coronary syndrome.
   - Adolescent with allergic reaction requiring urgent evaluation.
   - Critical case of an elderly patient with seizures and consciousness impairment.
   - Diabetic ketoacidosis in a young male.
   - Acute coronary syndrome in a male patient with steroid use.
   - Pediatric cases involving respiratory symptoms, asthma, and suspected abuse.
   - Snake bite incident in a child requiring urgent medical assessment.

**Achievements:**
- Clarified the importance of prioritizing severity in risk classification.
- Identified key symptoms and prioritization needs across diverse clinical scenarios.
- Provided comprehensive insights into emergency medical response and prioritization.

**Pending Tasks:**
- Further exploration of effective communication strategies in emergency scenarios.
- Development of training materials for healthcare professionals on risk assessment protocols.
