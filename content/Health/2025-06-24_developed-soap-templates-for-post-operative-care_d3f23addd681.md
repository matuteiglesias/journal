## Description

### Session Goal
The session aimed to develop and refine SOAP note templates for documenting post-operative care in various surgical scenarios, ensuring clarity and compliance with medical [[documentation]] standards.

### Key Activities
- Analyzed the legal requirements for displaying medical rosters in emergencies in Brazil, focusing on transparency and privacy issues between public and private sectors.
- Created structured SOAP notes for post-operative patients, including those recovering from vaginal hysterectomy and epididymal cyst surgery.
- Developed standardized terminology for describing increased bleeding, enhancing precision in medical communication.
- Documented the post-operative evolution of patients using the SOAP format, covering subjective and objective observations, assessments, and care plans.
