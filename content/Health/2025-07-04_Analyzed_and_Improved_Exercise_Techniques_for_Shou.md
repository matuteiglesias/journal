### Session Goal
The session aimed to evaluate and improve shoulder exercise techniques, specifically focusing on the 'Empujar el suelo boca arriba' and introducing more effective alternatives.

### Key Activities
- **Analysis of 'Empujar el suelo boca arriba':** The exercise was analyzed for its biomechanical inefficacy, with suggestions to replace it with more effective exercises like band pull-apart, Reverse Snow Angels, and Prone Y-T-W raises.
- **Discussion on Reverse Snow Angels:** Detailed the benefits and precautions of performing Reverse Snow Angels with added weight, and provided a progression plan for incorporating it into a training routine.
- **Instructional Illustration:** Created a two-panel illustration for the Reverse Snow Angel exercise, detailing correct body posture and arm movement.
- **Execution Guide:** Developed a guide for performing a two-phase arm lift exercise, emphasizing posture and weight usage.

### Achievements
- Identified and suggested more effective shoulder exercises.
- Developed comprehensive guides and illustrations to support proper exercise execution.
