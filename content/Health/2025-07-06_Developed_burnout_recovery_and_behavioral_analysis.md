### Session Goal
The session aimed to develop structured frameworks for diagnosing burnout and analyzing behavioral patterns for self-improvement.

### Key Activities
- **Burnout Diagnostic and Recovery Map**: Created a guide to diagnose and recover from burnout using targeted questions across three phases: Visible Pressures, Invisible Drains, and Unmet Needs & Misalignments.
- **Exploring Behavioral Patterns**: Developed a structured approach to uncover self-sabotaging behaviors through probing questions aimed at revealing underlying beliefs and emotional patterns.
- **Simulated Mental Pattern Audit**: Conducted a simulated introspective response to address behavioral patterns and cognitive beliefs, along with a disruption plan to combat avoidance and shame.
- **Streamlining User Inputs for [[Automation]] Systems**: Discussed strategies to simplify user inputs in automation systems to enhance usability and productization, outlining a framework to reduce complexity while maintaining functionality.
