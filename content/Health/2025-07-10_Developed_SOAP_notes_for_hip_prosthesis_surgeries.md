## 📅 2025-07-10 — Session: Developed SOAP notes for hip prosthesis surgeries

**🕒 14:55–16:00**  
**🏷️ Labels**: SOAP, Surgery, Hip Prosthesis, Medical Documentation  
**📂 Project**: Health  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


**Session Goal:** The goal of this session was to create structured SOAP notes for patients who have undergone hip prosthesis surgeries, focusing on both left and right hip replacements.

**Key Activities:**
- Developed a SOAP note template for a left hip prosthesis surgery, detailing the patient's status and care plan post-surgery. This included subjective, objective, assessment, and plan components.
- Created a similar SOAP note template for a right hip replacement (artroplastia de quadril direito), ensuring comprehensive documentation of subjective, objective, assessment, and care plan details.

**Achievements:**
- Successfully crafted detailed and structured SOAP notes for both left and right hip prosthesis surgeries, ensuring clarity and thoroughness in post-operative medical documentation.

**Pending Tasks:**
- Review and refine the SOAP note templates to ensure they meet all clinical documentation standards and are ready for implementation in patient care workflows.
