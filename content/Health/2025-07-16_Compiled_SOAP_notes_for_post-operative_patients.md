## 📅 2025-07-16 — Session: Compiled SOAP notes for post-operative patients

**🕒 14:05–14:15**  
**🏷️ Labels**: SOAP, Post-Operative, Clinical Records, Healthcare  
**📂 Project**: Health  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


**Session Goal:**
The session aimed to document the clinical progress of post-operative patients using the SOAP (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan) format, focusing on cases of post-hydrocelectomy and post-tubal ligation.

**Key Activities:**
- Utilized a structured template to record the clinical evolution of a patient who underwent right hydrocelectomy. The SOAP format was employed to detail subjective and objective findings, assessment, and post-operative care plans.
- Similarly, documented the immediate post-operative progress of a patient following tubal ligation, again using the SOAP format to ensure comprehensive coverage of the patient's condition and care strategy.

**Achievements:**
- Successfully created detailed clinical records for both cases, ensuring that all necessary information was captured in a standardized manner.

**Pending Tasks:**
- Review and verify the accuracy of the recorded information with the healthcare team to ensure compliance with clinical standards.
