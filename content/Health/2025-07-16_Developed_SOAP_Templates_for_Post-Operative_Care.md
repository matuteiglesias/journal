### Session Goal
The session aimed to develop structured SOAP (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan) templates for documenting post-operative care in various surgical procedures.

- Created SOAP notes for a range of post-operative scenarios including vasectomy, tubal ligation, postectomy, and urinary incontinence surgery.
- Developed clinical evolution records in SOAP format for procedures such as postectomia and histerectomia.
- Addressed network connection issues related to downloading Excel files and troubleshooting GitHub Actions network errors.
- Reviewed and improved lead management systems and developed strategies for reactivating contacts and adjusting prices in the real estate domain.
