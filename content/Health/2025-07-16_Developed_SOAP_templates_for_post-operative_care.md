## 📅 2025-07-16 — Session: Developed SOAP templates for post-operative care

**🕒 21:45–22:00**  
**🏷️ Labels**: SOAP, Healthcare, Post-Operative, Documentation, Bartholin, Vaginal Cyst  
**📂 Project**: Health  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


### Session Goal
The aim of this session was to develop and refine SOAP templates for documenting post-operative care in healthcare settings, specifically for surgeries related to Bartholin gland and vaginal cysts.

### Key Activities
- Created a SOAP template for post-operative analysis of Bartholin gland surgery, focusing on subjective, objective, assessment, and plan (SOAP) aspects.
- Developed documentation templates for the evolution of patients post-surgery for a right vaginal cyst, incorporating subjective and objective observations, assessments, and care plans.

### Achievements
- Successfully crafted detailed SOAP templates for two specific types of surgeries, ensuring comprehensive post-operative documentation.

### Pending Tasks
- Review and integrate these templates into the Tasy healthcare system for broader clinical use.
