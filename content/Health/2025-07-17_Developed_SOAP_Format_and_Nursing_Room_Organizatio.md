## 📅 2025-07-17 — Session: Developed SOAP Format and Nursing Room Organization Models

**🕒 12:45–16:30**  
**🏷️ Labels**: SOAP, Tasy, Nursing, Organization, Healthcare  
**📂 Project**: Health  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


**Session Goal:**
The session aimed to develop and refine templates for medical documentation using the SOAP format and to create models for organizing nursing rooms in healthcare settings.

**Key Activities:**
1. **SOAP Format Development:** Multiple iterations were made on creating a template for patient progress documentation using the SOAP (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan) format. This involved detailing subjective and objective patient information, assessments, and post-operative care plans.
2. **Nursing Room Organization:** Templates were designed for nursing staff to emphasize the importance of maintaining an organized and safe working environment. This included practical recommendations and clear instructions for maintaining order and collective responsibilities.

**Achievements:**
- Successfully developed a comprehensive SOAP format template for medical documentation.
- Created effective models for nursing room organization, ensuring a focus on safety and hygiene.

**Pending Tasks:**
- Further testing and feedback collection on the usability and effectiveness of the developed templates in real-world scenarios.

**Technical Insight:**
The session utilized structured template design to ensure clarity and usability in medical and organizational documentation, adhering to healthcare standards.

**Decisional Intent:**
To implement these templates in healthcare settings to improve documentation accuracy and workplace organization.
