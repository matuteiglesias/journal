## 📅 2025-07-14 — Session: Enhanced Job Evaluation and Filtering Prompts

**🕒 04:45–05:00**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Job Evaluation, Web Scraping, Ai Prompt, Job Search, Automation  
**📂 Project**: JobMarket  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


### Session Goal
The session aimed to refine and enhance prompts for evaluating job-related webpages and filtering job postings, focusing on improving user value and structured analysis.

### Key Activities
- **Refinement of Job Page Evaluation Prompt**: Developed a refined prompt for assistants to evaluate job-related webpages. The focus was on user value and structured analysis of scraped content.
- **Development of Job Posting Filtering Prompt**: Created a structured prompt to filter out low-quality or irrelevant job postings. This included specifying criteria for validity and reasons for rejection.
- **Revision of Job Match Assistant Prompt**: Amended prompts for job posting screening assistants, emphasizing quality evaluation and conditions for classifying postings as 'Not a Match'.

### Achievements
- Successfully refined the job page evaluation prompt, ensuring it focuses on user value and structured content analysis.
- Developed a clear and structured job posting filtering prompt to improve the quality of job search pipelines.
- Revised the job match assistant prompt with specific guardrails for better job matching accuracy.

### Pending Tasks
- Further testing and iteration on the refined prompts to ensure effectiveness in real-world scenarios.
- [[Integration]] of these prompts into existing job search and evaluation systems.
