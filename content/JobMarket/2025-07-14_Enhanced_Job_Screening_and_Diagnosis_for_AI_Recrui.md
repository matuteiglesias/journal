## 📅 2025-07-14 — Session: Enhanced Job Screening and Diagnosis for AI Recruitment

**🕒 05:05–05:15**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Job Matching, Ai Recruitment, Quality Assessment, Automation, Data Science  
**📂 Project**: JobMarket  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


### Session Goal
The primary objective of this session was to enhance the quality and effectiveness of job matching and screening processes, specifically targeting [[AI]] and data science roles.

### Key Activities
- Conducted a quality diagnosis of three job entries, assessing their summaries and raw inputs to identify strengths, issues, and areas for improvement in match scoring and classification accuracy.
- Revised the job screening prompt for an [[AI]] recruitment assistant, focusing on criteria to filter out low-quality job postings and emphasizing the importance of specific qualifications and experiences relevant to [[AI]] and data science roles.

### Achievements
- Identified key strengths and issues in current job matching processes, providing insights and recommended fixes to improve accuracy.
- Developed a refined prompt for job screening that is more aligned with high-quality match criteria, enhancing the recruitment process for [[AI]] and data science positions.

### Pending Tasks
- Implement the recommended fixes in the job matching algorithm to improve classification accuracy.
- Test and validate the effectiveness of the revised job screening prompt in real-world scenarios.
