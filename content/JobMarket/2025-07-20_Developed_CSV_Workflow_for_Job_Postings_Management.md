## 📅 2025-07-20 — Session: Developed CSV Workflow for Job Postings Management

**Session Goal:**
The goal of this session was to create a structured [[CSV]] table for managing job postings, specifically for data science and [[AI]] positions in Argentina.

**Key Activities:**
- Created a [[CSV]]-style table from shared job postings, including data on company names, locations, and job titles, and displayed it using a [[Pandas]] DataFrame.
- Developed a [[Pandas]] DataFrame containing job listings for data science and [[AI]] positions, focusing on company names, locations, and job titles.
- Formatted new job entries as a [[CSV]] file and explored the possibility of preparing additional entries with URLs or creating an automatic parser for HTML access.
- Created a second batch of job listings in a DataFrame for data science and [[AI]] roles, maintaining consistency in data fields.
- Planned for a second batch of job postings in [[CSV]] format, including fields for id, company, location, position, and a placeholder for URLs. Suggested creating an interface or script to manage and update the [[CSV]], and considered automating data extraction from LinkedIn.

**Achievements:**
- Successfully created and structured job postings data into [[CSV]] and DataFrame formats, facilitating better management and analysis.
