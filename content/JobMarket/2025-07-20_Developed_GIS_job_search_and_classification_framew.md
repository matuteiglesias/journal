## 📅 2025-07-20 — Session: Developed GIS job search and classification framework

**🕒 22:30–22:40**  
**🏷️ Labels**: GIS, Job Search, Classification, Data Management, Automation  
**📂 Project**: JobMarket  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


### Session Goal
The session aimed to enhance the job search process for GIS-related roles by developing a structured framework for job classification and application.

### Key Activities
- **Data Management**: Created a DataFrame of GIS job listings, including company names, positions, and locations, and saved it as a [[CSV]] file.
- **[[Job Search]] Structuring**: Implemented a workflow to filter GIS job listings by security requirements, experience level, job type, and location.
- **Job Role Classification**: Developed a script to classify GIS job titles into categories such as Analyst, Engineer, and Manager based on keywords, and counted the occurrences of each category.
- **Advanced Role Identification**: Identified top 10 GIS-related roles suitable for candidates with advanced technical skills, emphasizing strategic fit and skill transfer potential.
- **Technical Profile Evaluation**: Conducted a detailed analysis of technical profiles in relation to GIS industry standards in the U.S., including strengths, weaknesses, and strategies for obtaining H1B sponsorship from Argentina.
- **[[AI]]-Augmented Workflow Planning**: Outlined a strategic approach to job applications using automation and [[AI]] tools, divided into phases for efficiency.

### Achievements
- Successfully created a structured data management system for GIS job listings.
- Developed a classification system for GIS job roles, enhancing the ability to target specific job categories.
- Identified strategic job opportunities for advanced technical profiles.

### Pending Tasks
- Refine classification criteria for GIS job roles to improve accuracy and relevance.
- Implement the [[AI]]-augmented job application workflow in practice.
