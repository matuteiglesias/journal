## 📅 2025-05-28 — Session: Implemented Automated Economic News Monitoring System

**🕒 17:00–17:35**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Economic Monitoring, Automation, Rss Feeds, Inflation, Data Analysis  
**📂 Project**: Media  



### Session Goal:
The primary aim of this session was to design and implement an automated system for monitoring economic news, focusing on inflation and related economic indicators in [[Argentina]].

### Key Activities:
- Conducted an analysis of news articles concerning inflation in [[Argentina]], exploring content strategies and [[automation]] for daily processing.
- Developed criteria for filtering economic notes, emphasizing quantitative data and excluding non-numerical opinions.
- Established a framework for selecting relevant economic notes, focusing on inflation, reserves, debt, and international comparisons.
- Mapped an editorial schema to Google News RSS feeds, creating specific feeds for topics like inflation, exchange rates, debt, and employment.
- Provided a set of Google News RSS URLs for thematic economic monitoring, facilitating daily automated processing.
- Evaluated the performance of an economic information system, recommending technical improvements for content filtering.

### Achievements:
- Successfully designed a prototype for an automated economic news monitoring system.
- Created thematic Google News RSS feeds for targeted economic topics.
- Recommended enhancements to improve the system's ability to filter and process relevant economic content.

### Pending Tasks:
- Implement the recommended technical improvements to the economic information system to enhance filtering capabilities.
- Test and validate the automated RSS feed processing to ensure accuracy and reliability in news monitoring.
