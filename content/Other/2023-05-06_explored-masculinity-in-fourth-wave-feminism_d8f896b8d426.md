## Description

### Session Goal
The session aimed to explore the challenges and roles of men in the Fourth Wave of feminism, focusing on masculinity as a political project and the resistance to de-patriarchalization.

### Key Activities
- Reflected on the fragmented efforts by men to support feminist goals and challenge traditional masculinity.
- Analyzed the resistance of cisgender men to de-patriarchalization, identifying 'micromachist' practices as barriers to gender equality.
- Discussed the importance of questioning masculinity and male privilege to achieve gender equality.

### Achievements
- Gained insights into how feminist movements aim to subvert power structures that perpetuate oppression.
- Identified key factors hindering gender equality efforts, such as 'micromachist' practices and male privilege.

### Pending Tasks
- Further exploration of effective strategies to engage men in supporting feminist goals and overcoming resistance to de-patriarchalization.
