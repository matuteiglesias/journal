## 📅 2023-07-02 — Session: Addressed Psychological Violence in LGBT Events

**🕒 19:30–20:55**  
**🏷️ Labels**: LGBT, Psychological Violence, Diversity, Inclusion, Activism  
**📂 Project**: Other  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


### Session Goal
The session aimed to explore and address issues related to psychological violence and lack of diversity in LGBT events, focusing on creating safer and more inclusive environments.

### Key Activities
- Engaged in discussions on gender issues, specifically focusing on Argentine law and its impact on gender equality in public venues.
- Reflected on the lack of diversity in access control personnel at LGBT events and its implications for safety and fair treatment.
- Analyzed the effects of microaggressions, symbolic violence, and psychological violence on the LGBT community in event settings.
- Developed a manifesto calling for action against exclusion and violence in LGBT events, proposing demands such as diversity in control personnel, training, and anti-violence protocols.

### Achievements
- Clarified the connection between legal frameworks and gender issues in Argentina.
- Highlighted the importance of diversity and inclusion in event management to improve the experience of LGBT attendees.
- Produced a manifesto denouncing psychological violence and advocating for change in LGBT event practices.

### Pending Tasks
- Further dissemination of the manifesto to gather community support and signatures.
- Development of specific training programs and protocols to address psychological violence and promote diversity in event settings.
