### Session Goal
The session aimed to develop and refine creative prompts for generating visually appealing and thematic poster designs using Midjourney, focusing on both technical and whimsical themes.

### Key Activities
- Generated a list of creative Midjourney prompts for A0 poster designs, featuring themes like cosmic nebulae and cyberpunk cities.
- Created prompts for technical poster designs, including technical drawings and blueprints.
- Developed whimsical prompts for ant photography and humorous puppy images.
- Compiled creative ideas for wall posters, including minimalistic art and nature photography.
- Provided detailed descriptions for simulated window view posters, capturing city atmospheres.
- Revised and updated city window view descriptions for cities like Pisa and Barcelona.

### Achievements
- Successfully developed a diverse collection of creative prompts for Midjourney and poster designs.
- Enhanced descriptions for city window view posters, improving their visual and thematic appeal.
