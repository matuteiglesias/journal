## 📅 2023-11-21 — Session: Structured Plant Dataset and Care Guidelines

**🕒 03:00–04:00**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Plant Dataset, Botanical Family, Gardening, Plant Care, Ceropegia Woodii  
**📂 Project**: Other  



### Session Goal:
The session aimed to organize and enhance a plant dataset while providing comprehensive care guidelines for specific plant species.

### Key Activities:
- **Dataset Structuring**: Provided structured recommendations for naming columns in a plant dataset, focusing on clarity and relevance, including image filenames, common names, and botanical family names.
- **Botanical Insights**: Offered detailed botanical family information to aid in the completion of a plant varieties dataset.
- **Gardening Improvements**: Analyzed strengths and areas for improvement in a plant collection, suggesting diversification with floral, aromatic, air-purifying, aquatic plants, and bonsais.
- **Plant Care**: Listed delicate plants requiring special care and provided a comprehensive care guide for Ceropegia woodii, covering aspects like lighting, watering, humidity, temperature, soil, fertilization, pruning, propagation, and common issues.

### Achievements:
- Successfully structured plant dataset columns for improved [[data management]].
- Enhanced understanding of botanical family information for plant identification.
- Developed a detailed care guide for Ceropegia woodii.

### Pending Tasks:
- Further diversification of the plant collection as suggested, including the acquisition and care planning for new plant types.
