## 📅 2024-03-31 — Session: Developed interior design and art concepts for corridors

**🕒 03:35–04:00**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Interior Design, Art, Corridor, Modern, Botanical, Geometric  
**📂 Project**: Other  



### Session Goal
The session aimed to explore and develop interior design concepts and artistic themes suitable for corridors and entryways, focusing on modern and elegant aesthetics.

### Key Activities
1. **Interior Design Recommendations**: Proposed two design suggestions for a corridor: using monochromatic botanical line art on a yellow wall for a modern look, and geometric abstract posters in black and white to maintain visual interest.
2. **Artistic Corridor Design Concept**: Described a concept for a narrow house corridor featuring contrasting art styles on yellow and white walls, highlighting modern and organic aesthetics.
3. **Elegant Grayscale Art Themes**: Outlined themes for creating sophisticated grayscale artworks, including architectural elements and geometric shapes.
4. **Guide for Cohesive Art Theme**: Provided a structured approach to unify artwork throughout a home by defining a core theme, selecting a color scheme, and ensuring consistent art styles.
5. **Photography and Visual Design Suggestions**: Imagined a photograph of a 1990s Southern European apartment staircase and suggested visual design for entryways, inspired by vintage photography styles.

### Achievements
- Developed comprehensive design concepts and artistic themes for corridors and entryways.
- Created guidelines for cohesive art theming in home decor.
