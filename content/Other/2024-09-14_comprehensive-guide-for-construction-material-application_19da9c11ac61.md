## Description

### Session Goal
The session aimed to compile a comprehensive set of guides and plans for the application and [[optimization]] of various construction materials, focusing on waterproofing and surface preparation.

### Key Activities
- **Sika MonoTop®-107 Application**: Detailed instructions for surface preparation, mixing, application, curing, and post-application care.
- **Material Purchase [[Optimization]]**: Analysis of construction material purchases with recommendations for cost-saving and efficient use.
- **Waterproofing and Self-Leveling Grout Application**: Step-by-step guide for applying waterproofing membranes and self-leveling grout, including preparation and care.
- **Crack Repair Before Finishing**: Instructions for repairing cracks with mortar before applying finishing products like waterproof membranes or grout.
- **Cement Floor Finishing**: Guide on completing a smooth cement floor with polishing, microcement application, and sealing.
- **Roof Repair Plan**: Step-by-step plan to repair roof leaks, including membrane removal, cleaning, crack repair, priming, and waterproofing.
- **Advances in Waterproofing Technology**: Reflection on the superiority of modern waterproofing products over traditional asphalt membranes in terms of durability and ease of application.
- **Roof Restoration and Repair Guide**: Detailed plan for restoring and repairing deteriorated roofs, covering materials, tools, and procedures.

### Achievements
- Compiled detailed guides and plans for various construction material applications and repairs.
- Provided insights into the [[optimization]] of material purchases and the adoption of advanced waterproofing technologies.

### Pending Tasks
