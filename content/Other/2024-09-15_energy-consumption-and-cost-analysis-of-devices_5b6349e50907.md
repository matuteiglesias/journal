## Description

### Session Goal
The session aimed to analyze the energy consumption and cost efficiency of various devices, including monitors, fridges, and light bulbs, to provide insights into their energy usage and potential cost savings.

### Key Activities
- Assessed the compatibility of a solvent-based decorative brick protector with a 500W spray gun, considering factors like viscosity and dilution for optimal application.
- Extracted technical information about a monitor connected to an Ubuntu system using terminal commands, including EDID and hardware details.
- Estimated the energy consumption and monthly cost of the HP V270 monitor, considering specific electricity pricing.
- Analyzed the energy consumption and cost of a 1995 frigobar fridge, highlighting inefficiencies compared to modern models and calculating monthly energy costs.
- Conducted a detailed analysis of the energy consumption and monthly costs of various LED bulbs, emphasizing their energy efficiency compared to incandescent and compact fluorescent bulbs.

### Achievements
- Gathered comprehensive data on energy consumption for different devices, providing a basis for cost-saving decisions and efficiency improvements.
