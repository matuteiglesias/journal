## Description

### Session Goal:
The session aimed to explore exercises related to Hermitian matrices, positive definite matrices, and their orthonormal diagonalization, including conditions for symmetry and eigenvalues.

### Key Activities:
- Analyzed the conditions for matrix symmetry and eigenvalues, focusing on the determinant of the characteristic polynomial.
- Evaluated eigenvalues for different values of α, identifying α = 2 as the condition where λ = 0 is an eigenvalue.
- Detailed the process for orthonormal diagonalization of matrix A for α = 2, including obtaining eigenvalues and eigenvectors.
- Explored the Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) of a matrix, covering the calculation of singular values, singular vectors, and related properties.

### Achievements:
