## 📅 2025-04-11 — Session: Standardized Nursing Evolution Reports for TASY System

**🕒 07:55–08:40**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Nursing, TASY, Patient Care, Documentation, Healthcare  
**📂 Project**: Other  



### Session Goal
The session aimed to create standardized nursing evolution reports for patients in the TASY system, focusing on elderly and post-operative care.

### Key Activities
- Developed detailed clinical evolution descriptions for elderly patients with various conditions such as dyspnea and obesity, ensuring adherence to healthcare protocols.
- Created structured [[documentation]] for nursing evolutions in the TASY system, including technical records and care plans for patients with multiple neoplasias and those in palliative care.
- Revised and standardized nursing evolution reports for post-operative cases, including hip and knee arthroplasty, and hysterectomy, ensuring comprehensive coverage of patient conditions, vital signs, and care plans.

### Achievements
- Successfully produced a series of standardized templates for nursing evolution reports, tailored to different patient conditions and stages, compatible with the TASY system.
- Enhanced the [[documentation]] process by integrating clinical protocols and care plans, facilitating better patient monitoring and care.

### Pending Tasks
- Further refinement of templates to include more specific interventions for complex cases.
- [[Integration]] of feedback from healthcare professionals to optimize the [[documentation]] process.
