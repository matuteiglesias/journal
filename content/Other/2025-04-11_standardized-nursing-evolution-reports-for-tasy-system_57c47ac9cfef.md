## Description

### Session Goal
The session aimed to create standardized nursing evolution reports for patients in the TASY system, focusing on elderly and post-operative care.

### Key Activities
- Developed detailed clinical evolution descriptions for elderly patients with various conditions such as dyspnea and obesity, ensuring adherence to healthcare protocols.
