## Description

### Session Goal
The goal of this session was to develop and refine the SOAP (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan) model for documenting patient evolution in post-operative scenarios using the Tasy healthcare system.

### Key Activities
- Adapted the SOAP model specifically for post-operative immediate care, focusing on procedures like conization, hysterectomy, marsupialization of Bartholin's gland, and hernioplasty.
- Standardized the language and format for [[documentation]] across various surgical procedures to ensure clarity and consistency.
- Integrated nursing and clinical [[documentation]] practices into the SOAP model to enhance the quality of patient records.
- Detailed the subjective and objective states, assessments, and care plans for patients undergoing surgeries such as varicose vein treatment and urinary incontinence correction.

### Achievements
- Successfully developed a comprehensive and standardized SOAP [[documentation]] model for the Tasy system.
- Enhanced the [[documentation]] process by integrating nursing evaluations and care plans, ensuring a holistic approach to patient care.

### Pending Tasks
- Further refinement of the model may be needed to cover additional surgical procedures and post-operative care scenarios.
- Continuous feedback from healthcare professionals using the model to ensure its effectiveness and applicability.
