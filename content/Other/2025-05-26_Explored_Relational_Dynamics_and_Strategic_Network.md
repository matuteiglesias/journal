## 📅 2025-05-26 — Session: Explored Relational Dynamics and Strategic Networks

**🕒 21:40–22:10**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Game Design, Strategic Networking, Power Dynamics, Relational Games  
**📂 Project**: Other  



### Session Goal
The session aimed to explore and refine concepts related to relational dynamics, strategic [[networking]], and game design, with a focus on understanding interpersonal connections and power structures.

### Key Activities
- **Game Design for 'Pasar los 50'**: Developed a relational game concept using cards and dice to encourage interpersonal engagement.
- **CRM as a Living Deck of Cards**: Proposed a metaphorical framework for CRM systems, viewing contacts as a dynamic deck of cards.
- **Mazo Diario de Vínculos**: Designed a daily card game to foster reflection and responsibility in relationships.
- **Desafío Diario de Exposición Vincular**: Created a structured challenge for emotional hygiene through conscious interaction.
- **Analyzing Strategic Relational Networks**: Explored the dynamics of strategic networks, emphasizing relationships as latent assets.
- **Refining Power Dynamics**: Critiqued and refined the understanding of power dynamics in organizational behavior.
- **Radiography of a Political Actor**: Analyzed the cognitive and strategic traits of a political actor.
- **Understanding Policy Practitioners**: Unpacked the role of high-leverage, low-allegiance policy practitioners.
- **Liberating Your Trajectory**: Developed a framework for personal network renewal and contact management.

### Achievements
- Developed multiple frameworks and concepts for relational games and strategic [[networking]].
- Enhanced understanding of power dynamics and strategic relational networks.
