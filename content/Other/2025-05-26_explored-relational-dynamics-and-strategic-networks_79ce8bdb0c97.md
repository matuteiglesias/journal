### Session Goal
The session aimed to explore and refine concepts related to relational dynamics, strategic [[networking]], and game design, with a focus on understanding interpersonal connections and power structures.

- **Mazo Diario de Vínculos**: Designed a daily card game to foster reflection and responsibility in relationships.
- **Desafío Diario de Exposición Vincular**: Created a structured challenge for emotional hygiene through conscious interaction.
- **Analyzing Strategic Relational Networks**: Explored the dynamics of strategic networks, emphasizing relationships as latent assets.
- **Refining Power Dynamics**: Critiqued and refined the understanding of power dynamics in organizational behavior.
