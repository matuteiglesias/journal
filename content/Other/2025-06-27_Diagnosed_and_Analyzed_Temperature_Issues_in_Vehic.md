### Session Goal:
The session aimed to diagnose and analyze temperature-related issues in vehicles, focusing on sensor malfunctions and their impact on vehicle performance.

### Key Activities:
- Conducted a detailed analysis of symptoms and potential causes of cold start problems and erratic temperature readings in vehicles.
- Explored the functionality of cooling fans in relation to engine temperature, proposing an experiment to test the engine temperature sensor (CTS) for faults.
- Reflected on a case of incorrect temperature readings in a cold engine, identifying a faulty or disconnected CTS sensor as the likely cause.
- Provided recommendations for a compatible engine temperature sensor for the Peugeot 207, including market alternatives and replacement steps.
- Analyzed the indirect effects of oil addition on temperature readings, considering factors like overfilling and incorrect oil type.

### Achievements:
- Clarified the diagnostic process for temperature issues in vehicles, particularly focusing on the CTS sensor's role.
- Identified specific actions to confirm and address sensor-related faults.
