### Session Goal
The session aimed to explore and plan a strategic wardrobe system that leverages existing clothing to project various personal and professional personas, with a focus on strategic self-presentation and budget-friendly outfit management.

### Key Activities
- **Strategic Wardrobe Simulation**: Developed a framework for utilizing existing clothing items to project different personas without the need for purchasing new clothes. This approach emphasizes self-presentation.
- **Rotational Capsule Wardrobe Plan**: Created a low-budget outfit rotation system. This structured approach aids in daily outfit selection based on contexts and identities, optimizing the use of current wardrobe items.

### Achievements
- Established a strategic wardrobe framework that can be applied to enhance personal branding through clothing.
- Developed a practical and budget-friendly capsule wardrobe plan that facilitates efficient outfit planning.

### Pending Tasks

- Implementation and testing of the capsule wardrobe plan in real-world scenarios to assess its practicality and adaptability.
