## 📅 2025-07-17 — Session: Developed Guidelines for Narrative Word Selection

**🕒 01:10–01:20**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Narrative, Creative Writing, Storytelling, Word Selection  
**📂 Project**: Other  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


### Session Goal
The session aimed to establish criteria and guidelines for selecting impactful words in narrative design, and to construct a brief and effective personal narrative.

### Key Activities
- **Criteria for Choosing a Narrative Word**: Explored criteria for selecting a single, impactful word to generate narrative tension. Suggestions were provided based on different conceptual families.
- **Construcción de una Narrativa Breve y Efectiva**: Developed a guide for creating engaging personal narratives, emphasizing a striking start, development with contradictions, and an intriguing conclusion.

### Achievements
- Created a structured guide for word selection in narrative design.
- Developed a template for constructing personal narratives that capture attention.

### Pending Tasks
- Further refinement of the narrative word selection criteria to include more diverse conceptual families.
- Additional examples and templates for different narrative styles.
