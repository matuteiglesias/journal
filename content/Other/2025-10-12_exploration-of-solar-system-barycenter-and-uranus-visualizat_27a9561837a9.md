## Description

### Session Goal:
The session aimed to explore astrophysical concepts related to the Solar System's barycenter and the surface definition of gas and ice giants, as well as to conceptualize art and [[visualization]] ideas for Uranus.

### Key Activities:
- **Understanding the Solar System Barycenter Effect:** Explored how the Sun's rotation around the barycenter affects stellar dynamics and its implications for binary stars and exoplanets.
- **Understanding the Sun's Barycentric Motion:** Discussed the Sun's motion around the Solar System's barycenter, its impact on stellar properties, and planetary habitability.
- **Surface Definition of Gas and Ice Giants:** Clarified the concept of 'surface' for gas and ice giants like Uranus, defined at 1 bar atmospheric pressure.
- **Concept Art and [[Visualization]] of Uranus:** Developed ideas for visualizing a dark supercritical ocean on Uranus, focusing on atmospheric and fluid dynamics.
