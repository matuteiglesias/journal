## 📅 2025-10-18 — Session: Developed Plant Care and Photography Guides

**🕒 14:15–16:20**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Plant Care, Photography, Gardening, Templates, Visual Guides  
**📂 Project**: Other  



### Session Goal
The session aimed to develop structured guides for plant care and photography, focusing on organizing plant species based on care needs and creating professional photography prompts.

### Key Activities
- **Plant Grouping and Care Logic:** Developed a clustering system for plant species based on care requirements, including light, water, and soil preferences, organized into 12 distinct sets.
- **Care Guide Templates:** Created templates for plant care, detailing conditions such as light, watering, substrate, and ideal location.
- **Visual Guides:** Proposed a visual approach using emojis and iconography for plant care instructions.
- **Photography Prompts:** Generated detailed prompts for professional photography of indoor and outdoor plants, focusing on various plant types such as succulents, tropical plants, and herbs.

### Achievements
- Completed a comprehensive plant care guide with 12 standardized templates.
- Developed multiple photography prompts for professional plant photography, enhancing visual representation strategies.
