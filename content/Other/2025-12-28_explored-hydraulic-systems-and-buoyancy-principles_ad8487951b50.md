## Description

### Session Goal
The session aimed to explore various principles and design considerations related to hydraulic systems, buoyancy structures, and optical illusions in aquatic environments.

### Key Activities
- **Refraction-Based Aquarium Illusion**: Explored the mechanics of the aquarium illusion using refraction optics and observer viewpoint constraints.
- **Diving Bell Principle**: Discussed the hydrostatic principles behind the diving bell, its applications, and common misconceptions.
- **Buoyant Diving-Bell Dome Design**: Outlined design principles for constructing a buoyant diving-bell dome with emphasis on stability and air retention.
- **Hydraulic Connection Options**: Detailed methods for achieving different water heights in connected pools, including considerations for mechanical stability.
- **Static Equilibrium in Pools**: Explained conditions for static equilibrium in connected pools without pumps.
- **Sealed Headspace for Pressure Offset**: Discussed the use of sealed headspace to maintain different water levels in connected pools.
- **Design Considerations for Sealed Headspace**: Outlined pressure, stability, and safety considerations for sealed headspace systems.
- **DIY Hydraulic System Design**: Provided a detailed design and commissioning procedure for a DIY hydraulic system.
- **DIY Pressure Control System**: Outlined a DIY approach to creating a pressure control system using common materials.
- **DIY Hydrostatic System Diagram**: Presented a technical blueprint for a DIY hydrostatic system.

### Achievements
- Clarified the mechanics and misconceptions of the aquarium illusion and diving bell principle.
- Developed comprehensive guides for DIY hydraulic systems and buoyancy structures.

### Pending Tasks
- Implement and test the DIY hydraulic and buoyancy systems to validate theoretical designs and ensure practical feasibility.
