## 📅 2023-05-15 — Session: Developed curriculum framework for introductory computer science course

**🕒 06:05–06:15**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Curriculum, Computer Science, Education, Course Design, Programming  
**📂 Project**: Teaching  



### Session Goal
The aim was to develop a comprehensive curriculum framework for an introductory computer science course, combining both general and specific topics.

### Key Activities
- Reviewed and compiled a list of course topics, covering areas from hardware and software to artificial intelligence and human-computer interaction.
- Developed a structured framework in both English and Spanish, detailing thematic chapters that include fundamental concepts, control structures, data manipulation, and modern software development.
- Proposed a thematic structure divided into four chapters, addressing programming fundamentals, program analysis, technology and society, and systems and applications fundamentals.

### Achievements
- Successfully outlined a curriculum framework for a Computer Science 101 course, ensuring it is adaptable to both English and Spanish-speaking audiences.

### Pending Tasks
- Further refinement of the curriculum content to include specific learning objectives and assessment methods.
