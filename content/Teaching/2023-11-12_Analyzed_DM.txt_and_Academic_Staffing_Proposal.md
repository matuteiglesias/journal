**🕒 17:15–17:30**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Análisis, Dm.Txt, Propuesta Académica, Matemáticas, Docencia

### Session Goal
The session aimed to analyze the 'DM.txt' file and review the academic staffing proposal by the Department of Mathematics for the 2023 Ad-Hoc Commission.

### Key Activities
- Analyzed the content of 'DM.txt' focusing on data insights and implications.
- Reviewed the proposal from the Department of Mathematics, which requested additional staff due to retirements and resignations.
- Reflected on the vacancies in the Department of Mathematics and their impact on departmental experience.
- Discussed the importance of maintaining excellence in teaching and research in Algebraic Geometry, Homological Algebra, and Statistics, noting the negative impact of retirements.
- Examined the coordination efforts between the Department of Mathematics and the Institute of Calculus for staffing in statistics and the introduction of new courses for the [[Data Science]] degree.

### Achievements
- Gained insights into the staffing challenges and strategic needs of the Department of Mathematics.
- Identified the critical areas of teaching and research affected by staff changes.
