## 📅 2023-11-15 — Session: Developed and Revised Database Schemas for Academic Management

**🕒 14:15–17:56**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Database Design, Academic Management, Data Management, Er Diagram, DBML, Graphviz  
**📂 Project**: Teaching  



### Session Goal
The session aimed to explore and refine database schema designs for academic management systems, focusing on relational database structures and object sharing across multiple databases.

### Key Activities
- **Estructuración y Compartición de Objetos**: Explored structuring objects in relational databases for shared use, relevant in educational and research contexts.
- **Esquema de Relaciones**: Designed relational schemas for academic management using entity relationships, focusing on connections between teachers, students, courses, and publications.
- **Diagrama ER Creation**: Developed an Entity-Relationship diagram using Graphviz to visualize academic management relationships.
- **Schema Refinement**: Revised database schemas using DBML, correcting relationship errors and ensuring accurate many-to-many mappings.
- **[[Data Management]] Planning**: Drafted guidelines and revised articles for a [[Data Management]] Plan (PMD) working group, focusing on requirements, access policies, and collaboration protocols.

### Achievements
- Completed the creation and refinement of database schemas for academic management.
- Developed comprehensive guidelines for a [[Data Management]] Plan, including roles and protocols for deadline extensions.

### Pending Tasks
- Further review and testing of the revised database schema to ensure all academic management needs are met.
- Implementation of the [[Data Management]] Plan guidelines and protocols in the relevant academic departments.
