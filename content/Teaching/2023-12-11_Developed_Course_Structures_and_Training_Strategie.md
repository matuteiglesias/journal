## 📅 2023-12-11 — Session: Developed Course Structures and Training Strategies

**🕒 05:00–05:40**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Education, Training, Data Management, Cybersecurity, Public Safety  
**📂 Project**: Teaching  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


### Session Goal:
The session aimed to develop comprehensive structures for educational courses and training programs, with a focus on data management, cybersecurity, [[AI]], and public safety.

### Key Activities:
1. **Course Structure Proposal**: Designed frameworks for courses in data management, statistical analysis, cybersecurity, [[AI]], and machine learning, tailored to various knowledge levels.
2. **Training [[Strategy]] Development**: Formulated strategies to adapt police training programs to the diverse geography and socioeconomic conditions of Buenos Aires, incorporating virtual learning, regional centers, and content personalization.
3. **Evaluation and Improvement Plans**: Identified key aspects for evaluating and enhancing existing training centers and programs, specifically in technical fields like criminalistics and public security.
4. **Program Design for Public Safety**: Outlined a higher technical degree program in data analysis for public safety, integrating IT, cybersecurity, and soft skills.
5. **Onboarding Recommendations**: Provided guidelines for new employees at the Ministry of Security, emphasizing organizational culture, relationship building, system evaluation, and strategic planning.
6. **CDO [[Communication]] [[Strategy]]**: Developed an initial communication strategy for a Chief Data Officer to establish their presence and agenda through ministry portals.
7. **Collaborator Network Proposal**: Suggested a diverse, interdisciplinary network for the CDO, including internal teams, IT personnel, legal experts, and operational units.

### Achievements:
- Established a foundational structure for various educational courses and training programs.
- Developed strategic approaches for adapting training to local contexts and enhancing program offerings.

### Pending Tasks:
- Further refinement of course content and training materials.
- Detailed planning and implementation of the proposed strategies and programs.
