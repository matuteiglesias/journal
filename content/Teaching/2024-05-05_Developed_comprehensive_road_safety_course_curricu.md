## 📅 2024-05-05 — Session: Developed comprehensive road safety course curriculum

**🕒 19:00–19:30**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Road Safety, Education, Curriculum Development, First Aid, Argentina  
**📂 Project**: Teaching  



### Session Goal
The objective of this session was to develop a comprehensive curriculum for a road safety [[education]] course tailored for Argentina, focusing on various aspects of road safety and accident prevention.

### Key Activities
- **Course Structure Planning:** Detailed proposal for a six-unit road safety [[education]] course, covering topics such as road risks, driver profile, vehicle maintenance, accident analysis, and traffic legislation.
- **Unit 1 - Road Risk:** Developed framework focusing on understanding and identifying road risks to enhance driving safety.
- **Unit 2 - The Driver:** Structured content on driver responsibilities, safe driving characteristics, and psychophysical factors affecting driving.
- **Unit 3 - Vehicle Factor:** Outlined the impact of vehicle condition on road safety, including maintenance and technical inspections.
- **Unit 4 - Accident Analysis:** Focused on analyzing road accidents, including case studies, statistics, and first aid measures.
- **First Aid Training:** Detailed essential first aid principles and practices for accident scenarios, including initial assessment and victim assistance.

### Achievements
- Successfully outlined a detailed curriculum for a road safety [[education]] course with interactive activities and practical applications.
- Provided insights into mandatory vehicle inspections and their role in road safety.

### Pending Tasks
- Further development of interactive activities and assessment methods for each unit.
- [[Integration]] of more case studies and real-life scenarios to enhance learning outcomes.
