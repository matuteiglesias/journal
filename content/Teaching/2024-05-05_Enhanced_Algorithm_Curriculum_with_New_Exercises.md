The session aimed to enhance the existing algorithm curriculum by identifying gaps and introducing new exercises to provide a comprehensive learning experience.

**Key Activities:**
- Thematic grouping of algorithm exercises into categories such as Backtracking, Dynamic Programming, Graph Traversal, and Divide and Conquer.
