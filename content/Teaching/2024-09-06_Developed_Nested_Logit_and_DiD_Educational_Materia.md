### Session Goal
The session aimed to develop educational materials and insights related to nested logit models in transportation and Difference-in-Differences (DiD) regression analysis.

### Key Activities
- **Nested Logit Models:** Reviewed essential topics and recommended textbooks for mastering nested logit models, focusing on transportation decision-making.
- **DiD Regression Problem Design:** Outlined a step-by-step approach to create a calculation-based problem for DiD regression, including sample data and questions.
- **DiD Error Analysis:** Presented DiD estimates with errors to challenge students in identifying mistakes and enhance error complexity.
- **Investment Analysis:** Analyzed investment options using Expected Utility Theory (EUT) and Cumulative Prospect Theory (CPT).
- **Feedback on Dynamic Decision-Making:** Provided feedback on dynamic decision-making problems, emphasizing backward induction.

### Achievements
- Developed comprehensive guides and templates for nested logit models and DiD regression problems.
- Analyzed investment options, concluding Asset B as preferred.
- Enhanced educational materials with error analysis and feedback.
