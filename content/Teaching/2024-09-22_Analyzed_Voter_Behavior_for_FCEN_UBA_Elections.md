## 📅 2024-09-22 — Session: Analyzed Voter Behavior for FCEN UBA Elections

**🕒 05:35–06:00**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Elections, Data Analysis, Voter Behavior, FCEN UBA  
**📂 Project**: Teaching  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


### Session Goal
The session aimed to analyze voter behavior for the FCEN UBA elections, focusing on graduate voters and their participation from 2021 to 2024.

### Key Activities
- Initiated an inquiry into the types of elections data available, focusing on voter turnout and party results.
- Conducted an analysis of elections data specifically for FCEN UBA, examining voter status from 2021 and participation in 2024.
- Outlined analytical methods to study the relationship between voter status in 2021 and voting behavior in 2024, including contingency tables and voter retention analysis.
- Created a contingency table summarizing voting behavior across two election years.
- Developed a new classification column categorizing voter behavior in 2021 and 2024 using six specific categories.

### Achievements
- Successfully categorized voter behavior using a newly created column 'Clasificación Voto'.
- Provided a basis for further analysis of voting trends between 2021 and 2024.

### Pending Tasks
- Further analysis of the data using the new classification to derive insights on voter retention and activation rates.
