## 📅 2025-03-10 — Session: Comprehensive Database Modeling and SQL Practice

**🕒 10:00–10:50**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Database Modeling, ERD, SQL, Education, Data Transformation  
**📂 Project**: Teaching  



### Session Goal
The session aimed to explore various aspects of database modeling and SQL exercises, focusing on designing Entity-Relationship Diagrams (ERDs) and converting them into relational models across different domains.

### Key Activities
- **Entity-Relationship Modeling**: Designed ERDs for various domains including cinema production, mail order, movie databases, conference reviews, grade books, online auctions, and sports leagues (baseball, football, hockey).
- **Relational Model Mapping**: Converted ERDs into relational models, identifying entities, relationships, primary and foreign keys, and normalizing data.
- **SQL Exercises**: Practiced SQL queries for managing exam grades and flight reservations, focusing on data transformation and analysis.

### Achievements
- Successfully created detailed ERDs and relational models for diverse domains, enhancing understanding of database design principles.
- Completed SQL exercises that reinforced skills in data manipulation and query formulation.
