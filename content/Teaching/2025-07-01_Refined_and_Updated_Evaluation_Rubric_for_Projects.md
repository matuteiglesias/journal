### Session Goal
The primary goal of this session was to refine and update the evaluation rubric for assessing projects, specifically focusing on Fashion MNIST and practical assignments (TPs).

### Key Activities
- **Refinement of Rubric**: Iterated on the existing rubric to enhance the precision of evaluation criteria for Fashion MNIST projects. The aim was to ensure compatibility with current evaluation axes while adopting a more diagnostic and fair approach.
- **Update of Rubric**: Provided an updated rubric in [[CSV]] format for evaluating practical assignments, incorporating sub-criteria and scoring. Additionally, a spreadsheet model was suggested to streamline the evaluation process.

### Achievements
- Successfully refined the evaluation rubric for Fashion MNIST projects with improved criteria precision.
- Updated the evaluation rubric for practical assignments, complete with sub-criteria and scoring, and prepared it in a [[CSV]] format.
