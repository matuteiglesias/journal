## 📅 2025-07-02 — Session: Evaluated Group Reports for Data Analysis Projects

**🕒 02:00–02:10**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Evaluación, Informes, EDA, Grupos, Análisis  
**📂 Project**: Teaching  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


### Session Goal
The goal of this session was to evaluate and provide feedback on the data analysis reports submitted by groups 13 to 18, focusing on their exploratory data analysis (EDA) and introductory sections.

### Key Activities
- Detailed analysis of PDF reports from groups 13, 14, and 15, including scoring, justifications, and specific corrections for sections such as EDA, binary, multiclass, and style.
- Summary evaluations of the corrected reports for groups 13, 14, and 15, focusing on the Introduction & EDA sections, with brief justifications for each evaluated item.
- Detailed evaluations of the Part 1 (Introduction and EDA) of reports for groups 16, 17, and 18, presented in [[CSV]] format, with scores and comments on their performance across various practical aspects.

### Achievements
- Completed evaluations of reports for groups 13 to 18, providing comprehensive feedback on their EDA and introductory sections.
- Generated [[CSV]] summaries for group performances, highlighting clarity and justification of methods used.

### Pending Tasks
- Further analysis and feedback may be needed for subsequent sections of the reports if not covered in this session.

### Technical Insight
The session involved critical reflection and execution stages, utilizing both tabular and [[CSV]] formats to ensure comprehensive evaluations.

### Decisional Intent
The evaluations aimed to enhance the quality of student submissions by providing clear, actionable feedback to guide their future work.
