## 📅 2025-08-16 — Session: Developed Framework and Schema for Educational Evaluation

**🕒 18:45–19:00**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Evaluation, Education, JSON, Schema, QA, Framework  
**📂 Project**: Teaching  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


### Session Goal
The session aimed to enhance the evaluation process for educational contexts by developing a framework for response evaluation and a [[JSON]] schema for annotation.

### Key Activities
- Developed a detailed framework for evaluating educational responses, focusing on aspects such as correctness, pedagogical techniques, cognitive levels, and quality flags.
- Created a taxonomy for categorizing responses and suggested a scoring rubric for assessing their effectiveness.
- Provided recommendations for improving future educational replies.
- Designed a [[JSON]] schema for annotating responses, aimed at enhancing quality assurance and facilitating book assembly processes.

### Achievements
- Successfully outlined a comprehensive framework for educational response evaluation.
- Established a [[JSON]] schema to support quality assurance and book assembly.

### Pending Tasks
- Implement the scoring rubric in educational settings to test its effectiveness.
- Integrate the [[JSON]] schema into the existing QA processes to validate its utility.
