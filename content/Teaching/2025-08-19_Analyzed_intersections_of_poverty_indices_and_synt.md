## 📅 2025-08-19 — Session: Analyzed intersections of poverty indices and synthetic data

**🕒 13:30–13:45**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Multidimensional Poverty Index, Autoencoders, Synthetic Data, Machine Learning, Research Trends

### Session Goal
The session aimed to explore the intersection of the Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI) with autoencoder methodologies and evaluate synthetic data's role in socio-economic applications.

### Key Activities
- Reflected on the emerging research landscape where MPI intersects with autoencoder methodologies, highlighting key studies and suggesting future research directions.
- Analyzed the evaluation of synthetic data quality and utility for socio-economic applications, identifying key archetypes, methods, and metrics.
- Examined the integration of machine learning techniques in synthetic census data generation, discussing critiques from official statistics and implications for policy and research.
- Reviewed a SERP focused on synthetic census machine learning representation learning in demography, highlighting various methodological and application trends.

### Achievements
- Gained insights into the intersection of MPI and autoencoders, and the potential for future research.
- Identified the importance of both generation and evaluation in synthetic data research.
- Clarified the role of machine learning in synthetic census data and its policy implications.

### Pending Tasks
