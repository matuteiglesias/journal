### Session Goal
The session aimed to improve the organization of a shared lab folder and explore the use of a [[CSV]] schema for research artifacts.

### Key Activities
- **Structuring the Shared Lab Folder**: Developed a structured approach to organizing a shared lab folder, focusing on a clear hierarchy and the importance of various artifact types. This facilitates better orientation, knowledge sharing, and project management.
- **Leveraging [[CSV]] Schema for Research Artifacts**: Explored the use of a [[CSV]] schema as a foundational element for generating diverse research artifacts, including visualizations, reading paths, and mentorship simulations.

### Achievements
- Defined a clear folder structure for lab organization that supports scalability and efficient project management.
- Identified potential uses of [[CSV]] schemas in creating valuable research artifacts.
