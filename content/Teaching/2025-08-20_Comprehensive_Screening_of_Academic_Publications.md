### Session Goal
The session aimed to systematically screen and categorize academic publications into 'Keep' and 'Discard' lists based on their relevance to various domains such as data visualization, data integration, machine learning, socio-technology, computer science, and statistical methodology.

### Key Activities
- Conducted a detailed screening of academic publications from multiple batches (120-139, 160-179, 180-199).
- Utilized criteria related to data visualization, semantic web, pattern recognition, socio-technological intersections, and applied computer science to determine the relevance of each publication.
- Compiled insights in the form of tables and checklists to facilitate decision-making.
- Focused on retaining articles relevant to statistical methodology and data science while discarding those lacking relevance.

### Achievements
- Successfully categorized publications from the specified batches, ensuring that only those with significant relevance to the specified domains were retained.

### Pending Tasks
- Compile a cumulative table of retained items from the screened batches for further analysis and integration into ongoing research efforts.
