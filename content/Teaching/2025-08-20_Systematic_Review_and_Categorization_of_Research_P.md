## 📅 2025-08-20 — Session: Systematic Review and Categorization of Research Papers

**🕒 03:00–04:06**  
**🏷️ Labels**: Data Science, Research Review, Document Evaluation, NLP, Machine Learning  
**📂 Project**: Teaching  
**⭐ Priority**: MEDIUM  


**Session Goal:** The session aimed to systematically review and categorize a large batch of research papers and articles, focusing on their relevance to data science, machine learning, NLP, and related fields.

**Key Activities:**
- Conducted a systematic cross-analysis of categories in studies, identifying key categories for automatic filtering and proposing a hierarchical decision map.
- Reviewed batches of articles (e.g., Lote 258–326, 392–445, 514–565) to determine which to maintain or discard based on relevance to data science and methodological contributions.
- Screened research topics for relevance to data science and machine learning, categorizing them accordingly.
- Evaluated documents for their methodological and applied value in data science.
- Conducted batch reviews of research papers, focusing on their contributions to advanced statistics, NLP, symbolic [[AI]], semantic interoperability, and other domains.
- Analyzed CONICET publications and mapped authors and affiliations in economic papers.

**Achievements:**
- Identified and retained key papers with methodological contributions in statistics and NLP.
- Proposed thematic summaries and decision maps for retained documents.
- Mapped key collaborations and affiliations in CONICET economic papers.
